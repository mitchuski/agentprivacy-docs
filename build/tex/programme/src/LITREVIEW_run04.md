# The normative strand: positioning map, threat verdicts, placements (run 04)

## Positioning map: the poles of the personal-data-value debate

### anti-commodification

**Anchors.** Radin 1987 (Market-Inalienability); Roessler 2015 (closest data-specific statement); Véliz 2020 (Privacy Is Power) and Véliz 2024 (The Ethics of Privacy and Surveillance, OUP); Satz 2010 (noxious markets); Custers & Malgieri 2022 and EDPS Opinion 4/2017 (legal-doctrinal twin); Abdulrahim & Famoroti 2019 (noxious-markets applied to data)

**Claim.** Personal data belongs to a non-market valuation sphere: pricing it is corrosive per se, trade in it should be banned outright (Véliz: data as toxic asset, vote-selling analogy), and EU fundamental-rights doctrine makes the underlying right non-waivable regardless of consent or payment.

**PVM relation.** PARTIALLY VINDICATES — with Véliz placed precisely. PVM structurally vindicates two of her claims: (1) inalienability of the stock — under R(t)-scoped disclosure the observer can never reconstruct the underlying record and the quality stake is non-transferable by construction, so the sale Véliz wants legally banned is impossible in PVM's construction, a stronger result than a prohibition; this also explains rather than contradicts Custers-Malgieri/EDPS non-waivability. (2) Collective stakes — her claim that one person's data betrays others is carried formally as the social-data externality (Bergemann-Bonatti-Gan 2022) inside the rent decomposition. PVM REJECTS her third claim, that nothing should therefore be priced: scoped disclosure within the retained stock is legitimately priced; Satz's noxious-market criteria fail to bite precisely because the stock never transfers; and formally, prohibition is dominated by bargaining-position remedies (Gu 2024; Acemoglu et al. 2022's market-shutdown result holds only under conditions, partially vindicating the ban pole without generalizing it). Radin is the frame-owner: her shared-not-sold, markets-at-the-periphery middle position is exactly the position PVM occupies — WP-14 can claim only its technical realization, not the structure itself.

### propertisation (and its critics)

**Anchors.** Already engaged: Laudon 1996, Samuelson 2000, Schwartz 2004, Purtova 2015, Prins 2006. New: Cofone 2022 (Beyond Data Ownership), Ritter & Mayer 2018 (licensing-not-ownership), Calabresi & Melamed 1972 (entitlement-rule taxonomy)

**Claim.** Assign transferable property rights in personal data (pro side); critics: ownership magnifies consent pathologies, cannot reach inferred data, is self-defeating, and data lacks a coherent legal res.

**PVM relation.** AGREES with the critics and goes further: transferable title is the wrong object because value sits in reconstructable/inferred content, which R(t) prices directly and property title cannot reach (answering Cofone's strongest point). Against Ritter-Mayer, PVM's retention is structural (reconstruction-bounded), not contractual licensing. Against Calabresi-Melamed, PVM's construction occupies a cell the taxonomy lacks natively: an inalienability rule on the stock combined with a price mechanism on scoped flow, where the inalienability is technological rather than a chosen legal rule.

### data-as-labour

**Anchors.** Arrieta-Ibarra et al. 2018; Posner & Weyl 2018

**Claim.** Data is undercompensated labour; the observer-subject value gap is unpaid wages; remedy is a data wage negotiated by data unions.

**PVM relation.** DISAGREES on the diagnosis: the gap is market-position rent (venue exclusion + social-data externality per Bergemann-Bonatti-Gan 2022), not unpaid marginal product, so a data wage misprices the problem. PARTIALLY AGREES on the institutional form: unions-as-pooling survives, but as a bargaining-position instrument over scoped disclosure, not a wage claim over an alienated product.

### fiduciary intermediation

**Anchors.** Delacroix & Lawrence 2019 (already engaged); Marinotti 2022 (data trusts lack a coherent property res)

**Claim.** Data subjects should act through intermediaries bound by duties of loyalty (trusts, cooperatives) rather than transacting bilaterally.

**PVM relation.** AGREES as the remedy instrument — PVM's remedy IS pooled bargaining via cooperative/fiduciary structures. Marinotti's res critique is absorbed rather than resisted: because the stock is structurally inalienable, PVM's cooperatives pool disclosure rights and bargaining position (which exist) rather than corpus title (which may not), answering the strongest objection to trust constructions.

### formal externality economics

**Anchors.** Bergemann-Bonatti-Gan 2022 (already engaged); Acemoglu et al. 2022 (Too Much Data); Choi-Jeon-Kim 2019; Ichihashi 2021; Jones & Tonetti 2020; Fallah et al. 2024; Gu 2024 and Sun 2026 (preprints); Bergemann & Wang 2021 (tutorial)

**Claim.** Correlated-inference externalities depress equilibrium data prices and produce excessive collection even under informed consent; the traded object is designed/scoped disclosure; nonrivalry supports selling use of a retained stock; remedies are mediation, bargaining-power reduction, or (under conditions) market shutdown.

**PVM relation.** AGREES AND BUILDS ON: this line supplies the externality half of PVM's rent decomposition and the formal case that the gap is structural rather than super-additive aggregation. Two attribution duties: Jones-Tonetti already gives the priced-use-of-retained-stock economic form (retention assumed, not enforced — PVM enforces it via R(t)); and Bergemann-Wang 2021 shows the pooling/cooperative remedy is anticipated inside the Bergemann line itself, so WP-14's contribution is the subject-side valuation carrying that remedy, not the remedy.

### normative collective privacy

**Anchors.** Viljoen 2021 (relational data governance — highest-priority gap); Taylor, Floridi & van der Sloot 2017 (group privacy); Fairfield & Engel 2015 (privacy as public good); Barocas & Nissenbaum 2014 (inference end-run); Ben-Shahar 2019 (data pollution); MacCarthy 2011 (earliest externality policy statement)

**Claim.** Privacy interests are supra-individual: data relations create population-level interests that individualist frameworks (propertarian or dignitarian) cannot represent; individual choice/consent fails; remedies are democratic governance, group empowerment, or Pigouvian regulation without subject compensation.

**PVM relation.** PARTIALLY VINDICATES: PVM concedes the supra-individual interest is real and identifies the social-data externality as its economic shadow, with pooling answering the group rather than only the individual — this meets Viljoen and Fairfield-Engel halfway. DISAGREES with Viljoen's implication that subject-indexed valuation is per se inadequate (R(t) is subject-indexed while the remedy is collective), with Ben-Shahar's no-subject-compensation Pigouvian frame (a foil, since PVM routes the externality's value back to subjects), and answers Barocas-Nissenbaum's end-run directly: R(t) is defined against adversary inference, so leakage through others' data is priced rather than scoped away.

### data-as-capital (critical political economy)

**Anchors.** Sadowski 2019 (When Data Is Capital)

**Claim.** Data already functions as a capital class — but accumulated by firms through extraction, without consent or compensation.

**PVM relation.** DISAMBIGUATES: PVM's 'distinct capital class' must be marked off from Sadowski's firm-held extractive capital in one sentence — PVM relocates the stock to the data subject and makes it structurally inalienable, answering the extraction critique rather than repeating the propertisation move Sadowski attacks.

## Threat verdicts

### Inalienability leg of CTR-LR-02 — NARROWED (high, 85%)

**Target.** Inalienability leg of CTR-LR-02: PVM as first to price a formal privacy quantity while holding the underlying stock structurally inalienable, pricing scoped disclosure within a retained stock.

**Restatement (what survives).** PVM is the first to price an ADVERSARY-RELATIVE, TIME-DRIFTING reconstruction bound R(t) as the disclosure price within a SUBJECT-HELD stock, and the first to theorize buyer-side non-reconstructability as structural inalienability of a capital stock — joining the Calabresi-Melamed/Radin inalienability category to a formal pricing metric — rather than treating non-transfer as a mechanism feature (DP-markets), a broker-held architecture (Li et al.), a worst-case adversary-agnostic budget (FL-Market's LDP), or an assumed retention (Jones-Tonetti). The broad wording ('first to price a formal privacy quantity with the stock non-transferred') is NOT defensible and must be dropped: Li-Li-Miklau-Suciu 2013 priced DP-based privacy loss with buyers structurally unable to obtain raw records (arbitrage-freeness IS a reconstruction bound on combined purchases), and FL-Market 2021 adds subject-side structural retention with a priced LDP level.

**Basis.** Refuter did its coverage duty: two strong candidates verified by fetch (arXiv:1208.5258, arXiv:2106.04384), six search families probed beyond the digest, and a precise absence report — no source prices an adversary-relative or drifting bound, and legal (Custers-Malgieri, Radin) vs formal (DP-markets) literatures each hold exactly one half of the join, a gap the sweeps independently confirmed ('no paper prices disclosure UNDER an explicit inalienability constraint on the stock'). Confidence capped below 90 because the session search budget expired before two planned probes (retention-framed selling-privacy auctions; identifiability/reconstruction-risk pricing), so the residue rests on a competent but not exhaustive absence.

### Externality integration — NARROWED (medium-high, 75%)

**Target.** Externality integration: carrying the social-data externality / collective privacy (formal: Bergemann-Bonatti-Gan; normative: Véliz, group privacy) into a subject-side capital valuation with a venue-exclusion rent decomposition.

**Restatement (what survives).** PVM's defensible claim is the TRIPLE JOIN plus the decomposition: engaging the normative collective-privacy canon (Véliz, Taylor-Floridi, Viljoen) and formal externality pricing inside a subject-side capital valuation, with the observer-subject gap decomposed into venue-exclusion rent plus social-data externality within a structurally inalienable subject-held stock. Every PAIRWISE join is taken and must be conceded: Garrido-Merchán 2025/26 (preprints) join group-privacy normative concern to Pigouvian externality pricing with redistributive compensation; Fallah et al. 2024 (refereed) pay subjects for both local and collectively-imposed privacy losses; Acemoglu et al. 2022 build an externality-adjusted mediated compensation scheme; Fairfield-Engel 2015 join collective privacy to welfare economics in argument-space. Separately, the pooling/cooperative remedy must be re-attributed: Bergemann-Wang 2021 anticipates it inside the formal anchor's own line, so WP-14 contributes the valuation that carries the remedy, not the remedy.

**Basis.** No found work performs the triple join or contains a venue-exclusion decomposition, and the nearest hit (Garrido-Merchán) lacks the capital-class framing, R(t), inalienability, and engagement with the normative canon — and is an unrefereed 2026 preprint. Confidence held to 75 because the refuter's own not-searched list is material: no citation-graph check of works citing both Véliz/Taylor-Floridi AND Bergemann-Bonatti-Gan, no post-2024 SSRN law-and-economics sweep, no non-English literature — exactly where a triple-join candidate would live. The Bergemann-Wang re-attribution duty stands regardless of any further search.

## Refuter outputs (covering art + absence reports)

### The inalienability leg of CTR-LR-02 — refuter recommendation: NARROWED

- [strong] Chao Li, Daniel Yang Li, Gerome Miklau & Dan Suciu, "A Theory of Pricing Private Data", ICDT 2013 / ACM Transactions on Database Systems 39(4) 2014 / CACM 60(12) 2017 (2013) — Prices noisy aggregate query answers as a function of accuracy while the buyer can never obtain the raw records by construction; owner micro-payments are tied to a formal DP-based privacy-loss quantity, and arbitrage-freeness is exactly a bound on what a buyer can reconstruct by combining purchased disclosures. This is pricing scoped disclosure within a stock the buyer never receives — the priced+non-transferred join, a decade before WP-14. Narrowing residue: the stock is alienated to a trusted broker (not subject-held), the bound is worst-case DP (not adversary-relative or time-drifting), and non-transfer is a mechanism feature, never theorized as inalienability or a capital class. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5258>
- [strong] Shuyuan Zheng, Yang Cao, Masatoshi Yoshikawa, Huizhong Li & Qiang Yan, "FL-Market: Trading Private Models in Federated Learning", IEEE BigData 2022 (arXiv:2106.04384, 2021) (2021) — Structural (architectural) inalienability plus priced formal privacy quantity in one mechanism: raw data never leaves the data owners (federated learning), owners locally perturb gradients under local differential privacy, and the perturbation/privacy level is what the auction prices. The subject retains the stock by construction; only scoped, formally-bounded disclosure is sold. Residue for WP-14: LDP epsilon is a worst-case, adversary-agnostic bound, not an adversary-relative time-drifting R(t); no capital-class or rent analysis. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04384>
- [partial] Guocheng Liao et al. (personalized-DP pricing line), "Pricing Private Data with Personalized Differential Privacy and Partial Arbitrage Freeness", arXiv:2105.01651 (2021) — Extends the Li-Miklau-Suciu construction to per-subject (personalized) privacy levels — each owner prices her own formal privacy loss while the buyer still receives only perturbed answers. Evidence the priced-formal-quantity-with-non-transferred-records design is a developed literature, not a single paper. Same residue: worst-case DP, broker-held stock, no inalienability theorization. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01651>
- [partial] Charles I. Jones & Christopher Tonetti, "Nonrivalry and the Economics of Data", American Economic Review 110(9): 2819-2858 (2020) — General-equilibrium precedent for pricing USE of a stock the consumer retains: nonrivalry means consumers sell use broadly while keeping the record, and consumer-held rights approach the social optimum. Covers the 'priced flow from a retained stock' economic form and the subject-held allocation, but has no formal privacy quantity and no reconstruction bound — retention is assumed, not enforced structurally. <https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20191330>
- [partial] Shota Ichihashi, "The Economics of Data Externalities", Journal of Economic Theory 196: 105316 (2021) — Formal precedent for the purchased object being designed/scoped disclosure rather than the underlying stock (information design over what the firm learns). - [partial] Bart Custers & Gianclaudio Malgieri, "Priceless Data: Why the EU Fundamental Right to Data Protection Is at Odds with Trade in Personal Data", Computer Law & Security Review 45 (2022) — Covers the inalienable/non-waivable leg from the legal side (GDPR/Charter rights cannot be alienated; any data-as-counter-performance stays revocable), i.e., a non-waivable stock coexisting with data transactions — but supplies no pricing metric, so it covers only one half of the join. <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0267364922000309>
- [partial] Margaret Jane Radin, "Market-Inalienability", Harvard Law Review 100(8): 1849-1937 (1987) — Owns the conceptual category the leg's novelty claim sits inside: things that may be shared but not sold, with markets permitted at the periphery — exactly WP-14's middle position, stated normatively. No pricing metric, so it covers the frame, not the join; but it means WP-14 cannot claim the shared-not-sold structure itself, only its technical realization. <https://people.brandeis.edu/~teuber/Radin-Market-Inalienability.pdf>
