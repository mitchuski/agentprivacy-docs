On 2026-07-10 the moving ceiling shipped as a live, display-only instrument across the three
soulbis lattice rooms (repo `soulbis website`, commits `b222937` "V6 across the rooms: the
moving ceiling becomes visible (C82)" and `e2cb7f8` "Ceiling epistemics pass"; chronicle

- **/lattice — the floodline.** A flood rises through the sovereignty strata; every vertex whose
  stratum fraction sits below R(t) is veiled bottom-up — stratum 0 first, V63 last, all 64 at
  breach.
- **/sigil — the closing ring.** A ring contracts from the canvas edge onto the 64-glyph
  rune-row; past t* it passes inside and the constellation dims: the sigil's guarantee has
  expired.

All three read the same model — R(t) = R₀·e^(g·t), t* = ln(1/R₀)/g, defaults r₀ 0.35 / g 0.25 —
carry the same badge register, and carry the same honesty label verbatim: *C82 · ~65% ·
mechanism evidenced (n=2), rate unparameterized · the register wins.* The ceiling renders in a
third color, neither Swordsman coral nor Mage cyan: the ceiling is not a party to the dyad.

Two epistemic corrections landed the same day (`e2cb7f8`) and are load-bearing for any
rehydration that describes the instrument:

1. **Breach language.** "Is readable now" was wrong and was replaced: past t* the correct claim
   is that *nothing in the mathematics prevents reading what was once out of reach*. The bound
   expires; no reading event is asserted.
2. **Retroactive drain.** The T_∫(π) tracer value accrued by walking the path does not freeze at
   breach — it *drains* (half-life ≈ 1.5 s in instrument time). Path value accrued under a
   ceiling is retroactively exposed when the ceiling passes, because the archive of the walk
   outlives the deficit condition. This is the instrument's one genuine conceptual contribution:
   E2-C01's "what expires at t* is the deficit condition, not the architecture" rendered as a
   consequence for *already-accrued* path value, not just future flow.

## 2. What an instrument is for, stated honestly

The instrument makes one thing cheap that the spec makes precise: *feeling the asymmetry of
t\**. Sliding g is frontier-capability drift the subject does not control; sliding R₀ is the
archive term the subject partially controls; watching the flood take the lower strata first is
the compression-spectrum ordering (§21) made kinetic. None of this is evidence for C82's rate.
The honest chain is: mechanism evidenced (n=2, spec §25) → rate unparameterized (E2-C04's
formalisation obligation stands) → instrument renders the *shape* of the conjecture so that the
rate question can be asked by non-specialists. A reading surface recruits witnesses; it does not
substitute for them.
