## Writing Guidelines

### For As By The Sword

- Use declarative statements: "The reconstruction ceiling IS..."
- Present tense for operational systems
- Past tense for proven theorems
- Include exact version numbers, line counts, empirical data
- Reference specific code locations where relevant
- Tone: Certain, bounded, architectural
- *Voice of the right hemisphere: broad attention, pattern recognition, what already exists*

### For As By The Mage

- Use conditional statements: "The mana economy COULD enable..."
- Future tense for projections
- Present progressive for emerging systems
- Include confidence percentages for conjectures
- Reference roadmaps and vision documents
- Tone: Projective, expansive, narrative
- *Voice of the left hemisphere: narrow focus, sequential reasoning, what could become*
