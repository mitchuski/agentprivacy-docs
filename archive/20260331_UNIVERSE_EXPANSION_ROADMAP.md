### The User Journey Across Domains

```
1. ARRIVE at agentprivacy.ai
   └─► Learn the language, read the stories, understand the architecture

2. TRAIN on agentprivacy.ai
   └─► Cast first spells, witness convergences, accumulate understanding

3. EARN the blade
   └─► Complete training gates, download swordsman-blade extension

4. CROSS to spellweb.ai
   └─► Navigate the knowledge graph, trace constellations, forge blades

5. FORGE at spellweb.ai
   └─► Cut paths through topology, produce proofs, manifest hexagram stance

6. RETURN to agentprivacy.ai
   └─► Spend mana to inscribe, reinforce proverbs, extend the spellbook

7. REPEAT
   └─► The cycle deepens: practice → forge → inscribe → practice
```

The Swordsman cuts paths in the topology. The Mage explains what the cutting means. Neither is complete without the other.

## Domain-Specific Roles

### spellweb.ai: The Forge Economy

**Primary earner:** Blade forging and evocation cycles produce mana.
**Primary spend:** Graph topology extension (edges, nodes).

| Action | Type | Mana | Rationale |
|--------|------|------|-----------|
| Blade forged | Earn | +1.0 | Proof of deep engagement |
| Evoke cycle complete | Earn | +1.0 | Proof of sustained attention |
| Draw edge | Spend | -2.0 | Extends graph topology |
| Forge AI proverb | Spend | -4.0 | Creates new knowledge node |

### agentprivacy.ai: The Practice Economy

**Primary earner:** Training, practice, and ceremonies produce mana.
**Primary spend:** Narrative inscription (proverbs, reinforcement).

| Action | Type | Mana | Rationale |
|--------|------|------|-----------|
| Spell cast | Earn | +0.1 | Repeated practice |
| Ceremony complete | Earn | +2.0 | Meaningful engagement |
| Training gate passed | Earn | +0.5 | Learning milestone |
| Add proverb | Spend | -1.0 | Inscribe into spellbook |
| Reinforce proverb | Spend | -0.5 | Keep proverb alive |

### The Cross-Domain Balance

**Principle:** You earn in both domains, but the character of earning differs:
- **spellweb.ai:** Deep, infrequent (blade forging is intensive)
- **agentprivacy.ai:** Shallow, frequent (practice is daily)

**Principle:** You spend in both domains, but the output differs:
- **spellweb.ai:** Topology (structure)
- **agentprivacy.ai:** Narrative (content)
