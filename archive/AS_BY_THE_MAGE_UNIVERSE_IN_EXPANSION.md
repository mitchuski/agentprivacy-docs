## 1. The Speculative Frontier (C6-C10)

Open questions the forge has not yet answered. Each conjecture carries a confidence level — the Mage's estimate of structural truth.

### C6: P^1.5 ↔ 96/64 Structural Correspondence (15%)

**The Conjecture:** The ratio 96/64 = 1.5 is structurally connected to P^1.5 — the superlinear privacy exponent carried since V2.

**What it would mean:** If true, the entire Privacy Value Model equation is an expression of the holographic principle applied to sovereignty architecture. The exponent is not a calibration choice — it is the geometry expressing itself.

**Status:** Numerically coincident. No derivation from first principles. The numbers match. We do not know if the matching is meaningful.

**Path to resolution:** Formal derivation showing P^1.5 emerges from boundary computation on the 96-edge surface. Or: empirical evidence that other exponents work equally well (falsification).

### C7: Path Integral Structure (25%)

**The Conjecture:** The path integral T_∫(π) correctly replaces additive edge sums. Correlations between sequential transitions matter for privacy value.

**What it would mean:** The forging sequence is not just a witness — it carries value. Two blades at the same vertex with different forging histories carry different T_∫ values. The dance matters, not just the destination.

**Status:** Theoretically motivated by BRAID reasoning graphs. No empirical comparison between additive and integral formulations.

**Path to resolution:** Construct scenarios where additive and integral predict different outcomes. Test empirically.

### C8: BRAID Compression Reduces R (45%)

**The Conjecture:** BRAID-style compression (74× token reduction) reduces the reconstruction ceiling R_max in practice, not just in theory.

**What it would mean:** Every token not sent is a token that cannot be intercepted. Compression is defence. The smallest proof is the strongest proof.

**Status:** Theoretically grounded (fewer tokens = smaller reconstruction surface). Strengthened by behavioural density observation. No formal proof connecting compression ratio to information-theoretic bounds.

**Path to resolution:** Formal proof. Or: empirical demonstration of reconstruction attempts on compressed vs. uncompressed inference traces.

### C9: Holographic Boundary Sufficiency (25%)

**The Conjecture:** Privacy value can be computed entirely from boundary observations. The 96-edge surface encodes the full 64-vertex bulk.

**What it would mean:** You never need to measure the interior. The boundary measurements are sufficient. This has profound implications for audit and verification — you could verify privacy without inspecting private data.

**Status:** Implied by the holographic principle from physics. No discrete lattice verification.

**Path to resolution:** Construct a verification protocol that operates only on boundary edges. Demonstrate equivalence to bulk measurement.

### C10: O(1) Shared-Parent Modifies k (20%)

**The Conjecture:** Agents sharing a reasoning library (guild members) coordinate at O(1) cost per member, modifying the effective network exponent k.

**What it would mean:** Guild structures scale better than individual agent networks. The shared parent (reasoning library) absorbs coordination cost. This could explain why communities with shared praxis coordinate more efficiently than atomised individuals.

**Status:** Structurally implied by BRAID architecture. No calibration against empirical guild performance data.

**Path to resolution:** Deploy guilds. Measure coordination overhead. Compare to predicted O(1) behaviour.

## 12. The Mesh Architecture

The network that serves sovereignty.

### Tailscale Integration

**What it is:** A mesh VPN built on WireGuard. Zero-config private networks.

**What it could enable:** The private mesh from Act XXV made deployable. Sovereign nodes communicating through encrypted tunnels without central servers.

### Control Plane ⊥ Data Plane

The same separation that runs through the architecture:

| Layer | Control Plane | Data Plane |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| Network | Coordination, discovery, routing | Actual data transmission |
| Agent | Swordsman (what to protect) | Mage (what to project) |
| Trust | VRC negotiation | Actual relationship operations |

**The separation must hold at every layer.** If control and data merge, the architecture collapses.

### DERP Relay Considerations

Tailscale uses DERP relays for NAT traversal. These are potential observation points.

**Mitigation:** End-to-end encryption means DERP sees encrypted packets only. But traffic analysis remains possible.

**Deeper solution:** Run your own DERP relay. Home sovereignty extends to network infrastructure.

### Home Sovereignty

**Vision:** Your home becomes your sovereign node. Your network serves your sovereignty. Your infrastructure answers to you.

**Requirements:**
- Always-on computing (Raspberry Pi, home server)
- Mesh VPN configuration
- DERP relay (optional but enhances sovereignty)
- IPFS/Filecoin node (data layer)

**Status:** Design exists. Implementation requires hardware deployment.
