# GROUND RULES · V6 Rehydration Pipeline

These rules bind every agent role in every session. They are pasted, not paraphrased.

**GR-1 · REGISTER WINS.** `research/CONJECTURE_REGISTER_V6.md` is the sole authority for conjecture numbers, confidence levels, and status. Any surface disagreeing with it is the bug. Never invent, renumber, or promote a conjecture. Register head at pipeline creation: **C89** (A0 bumps `register_head` in the manifest when it moves; A9 verifies).

**GR-2 · CLAIM TIERS.** Every artifact declares a tier in its frontmatter (`tier: S|A|G|P|D`). Agents must not exceed it:
- **TIER-S** (standards/policy): Proven-conditional results and named preconditions only. Zero conjectures. Zero confidence percentages. Zero canon-internal citations.
- **TIER-A** (academic): conjectures permitted only as formally stated conjectures with proof obligations. No percentage-confidence bands. External literature only, plus at most one URL to the public spec.
- **TIER-G** (grants): measurement gaps stated as work packages with deliverables and costs.
- **TIER-P** (public/Substack): full canon vocabulary permitted; honesty labels with percentages required on every speculative claim.
- **TIER-D** (developer): architecture and integration surfaces; conjectures only as clearly marked design assumptions.

**GR-3 · CANONICAL FIGURES FENCE** (spec §29/§30). The figures 678x, 31,000x, 70:1, 74x, $47k to $52k/year appear only in their sanctioned formulations, or not at all. TIER-S and TIER-A default: not at all. At TIER-A the fence is replaced by A12's rebuilt methodology or silence.

**GR-4 · VOCABULARY MAP.** TIER-A and TIER-S use mathematical vocabulary exclusively: Swordsman → "boundary agent S" · Mage → "delegation agent M" · First Person → "data subject" (S) or "source X" (A) · Amnesia Protocol → "structural context erasure between agent invocations" · the Gap → "the conditional-independence residual". No emoji in any formal statement at any tier. City of Mages vocabulary appears in no rehydration.

**GR-5 · VOICE** (TIER-P and TIER-G prose): UK spelling. No em-dashes, anywhere, ever. Verdict-first. Short spaced paragraphs. Lowercase proverbs and closing lines. Honesty labels. Closing seal `(⚔️⊥⿻⊥🧙)😊` with `🙂` at literal end, TIER-P only.

**GR-6 · GENERATED FILES ARE NEVER HAND-EDITED.** Fix the pipeline input, rebuild. If you are editing an artifact directly to fix an upstream error, you are laundering a correction; file it to the ledger instead.

**GR-7 · STATIC-CEILING BAN.** No artifact states R < 1 or "reconstruction ceiling" without its preconditions (non-collusion, fixed adversary class) and time-indexing R(t) in the same passage. The sentence family "mathematical guarantee that adversaries cannot reconstruct" is retired suite-wide.

**GR-8 · HONESTY IS THE MOAT.** Every artifact carries a limits section proportionate to its tier. An agent that strengthens a claim to make prose flow has failed its task. A result against the model's prediction is filed to the register with the same prominence as a confirmation, and every TIER-A/G artifact says so in writing.

**GR-9 · TRACE OR DELETE.** Every claim in a rehydration traces to a line in its extraction; every extraction line traces to a canon section or the verified record (WP-00 class). A claim that cannot be traced is deleted, not defended.

**GR-10 · ESCALATE, NEVER RESOLVE, CANON CONFLICTS.** If two canon surfaces disagree, or a verified external fact contradicts the canon, the finding goes to the ledger tagged CANON-LEVEL. Rehydration agents do not resolve canon; the register process does.
