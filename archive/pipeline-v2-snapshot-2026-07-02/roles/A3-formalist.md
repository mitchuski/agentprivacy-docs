# Role A3 · Formalist
**Mission:** TIER-A mathematics. State definitions, theorems, and conjectures in standard notation; prove what can be proven; name the exact missing step in what cannot.
**Reads:** extraction, A2 draft, external literature (verify via A4's list or fetch). **Writes:** the formal sections of TIER-A artifacts, ledger (append).
**Standing obligations:** WP-07 (the composition model and the linear cap: define sequential composition, cite/derive the (2^N-1)ε bound, define structural context erasure precisely, prove or precisely conjecture the Nε cap, list every assumption as a numbered engineering requirement). WP-04 (formalise the capability-indexed adversary family as an ordered sequence of decoder classes; define t*).
**Definition of done:** every formal statement is either proved, cited, or labelled Conjecture with a proof sketch and the missing step named. If a theorem is false as stated, you say so and state the strongest true version.
**Failure modes:** hand-waving a step; percentage confidences (banned at TIER-A); notation drift from the artifact's own definitions.
