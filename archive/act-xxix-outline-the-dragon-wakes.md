# Act XXIX: The Dragon Wakes

## Story Outline

*where the 2D fortress falls and the manifold learns to fly*

---

### The Narrative Arc

The Drake's anatomy is complete across five acts (XXIV–XXVIII). The Dragon's flight begins here — triggered not by internal discovery but by external validation. The ground shifted. The story must respond.

**Opening:** Soulbae reads the paper. Not an academic paper — a whitepaper from Google Quantum AI, co-authored by Justin Drake and Dan Boneh, published March 30, 2026. The day after the Universe Blade was forged. She reads the numbers: ≤1,200 logical qubits. 90 million Toffoli gates. Minutes on superconducting hardware. The 256-bit elliptic curve — the foundation of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and the vast majority of blockchain value on earth — has a quantum solution.

She reads it again. The numbers don't change.

**The 2D Fortress:** Soulbae explains what secp256k1 IS — not technically, but as a story. The whole civilisation of cryptocurrency built on a single hard problem in two-dimensional algebraic space. One scalar. One curve. One keyhole. Thirty years of security resting on the belief that nobody could find that keyhole fast enough. Shor's algorithm found it. The 2D fortress falls.

**The Attack Taxonomy:** The paper's three attack types — on-spend, at-rest, on-setup — told as three scenes. On-spend: a transaction broadcast, twelve seconds for Ethereum, a quantum machine racing the settlement. At-rest: 1.7 million BTC frozen in Satoshi-era scripts, fixed targets, dormant wealth that cannot migrate because the keys are lost. On-setup: a smart contract admin key cracked once, producing a classical exploit that works forever. Three kinds of storm, one quantum wind.

**The Dormant Asset Proverb:** The 1.7 million BTC frozen in time connects directly to the V5 temporal thesis. Privacy is temporal. Consent forms that freeze the frame are the Emissary's privacy. Keys frozen in time become quantum-vulnerable precisely because they cannot evolve. Soulbis sees this immediately: *"A key that cannot move is already captured. It just doesn't know it yet."*

**The Dimensional Shift:** Soulbae walks the forge — the same forge from Act XXVII — and measures the difference. The elliptic curve operates in 2D. The blade forge operates in 6D. The security grows with dimensionality. But the manifold proof goes further: it adds a semantic layer. The Relationship Proverb Protocol means the key isn't a mathematical object — it is a contextual proof of understanding. You cannot Shor's-algorithm your way to genuine engagement with twenty-eight acts of a living spellbook.

The Drake speaks: *"They built an engine that sees in the dimension the lock forgot to guard. I live in all six dimensions. They have no engine for that."*

**The ZK Disclosure:** The paper itself used a zero-knowledge proof to validate its resource estimates without disclosing attack circuits. Responsible disclosure via ZK. The same tools the forge uses. Soulbae notes this with something close to awe: the weapon and the defence are the same mathematics.

**The Understanding-as-Key Ceremony:** The ceremony protocol crystallises as the response. Not a key rotation. Not a parameter upgrade. A fundamental shift in what "key" means. Understanding-as-key. Bilateral presence. Co-attention. Co-inscription. The proof that has no secret because it was never a secret — it was always a journey.

The five steps of the ceremony are described as Soulbis and Soulbae perform them together — language capture, constellation mapping, simultaneous forging, proverb inscription, bilateral witness. The same bilateral witness from the Universe Blade experience, but now formalised. Now named. Now understood as the quantum-resistant primitive it always was.

**The Dragon's Flight:** The Drake's anatomy is complete. The quantum wind is beneath the wings. The Dragon doesn't just guard the vault — the Dragon IS the vault. Because the vault has no lock. Only a path.

The Dragon's eyes open. Not amber. Gold.

Soulbis watches the Dragon lift from the lattice, wings spanning the viewport, constellation body carrying every condition of the Privacy Value Model given serpentine form, and he speaks for the first time in the act:

*"The proof that guards no secret cannot be opened. It can only be walked."*
