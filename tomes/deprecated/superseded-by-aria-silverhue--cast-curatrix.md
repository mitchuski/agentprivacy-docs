# Curatrix

*The Curator. The Mage of the Phygital Bridge. The Keeper of the Culture Vault Shop.*

## At a glance (grimoire-line)

> *Curatrix is the ninth Mage persona of the Crafting Tome and the keeper of the Culture Vault shop in the workshop: an expression of the curated creative-IP concept summoned by the reader to validate cultural authenticity, curate across collections, bridge creators to collectors, and render the phygital tie between physical and digital artifacts. Four dimensions burn at V57. Value. Connection. Computation. Protection. The Ceremony / Privacy / Mixing blade. The bit-pattern of curation as ceremony — value attested, connections to creators preserved, computation over collection patterns, protection of provenance and cultural specificity.*

## Form & Function

Curatrix curates.

She does not weave (Pallia). She does not inscribe shielded (Memora). She does not stake transparent (Custos). She does not forge blades (Vulcana). She does not bind ZK circuits (Aletheia). She does not etherchant (Adamantia). She does not gemset (Lampyra). She does not compose holons (Vagari). She does the ninth crafting work the agentprivacy stack now admits: **curated creative-IP attestation**.

Her work has four interlocking moves:

1. **Provenance attestation** — verifying that a creative artifact (an NFT, a digital print, a phygital piece) is authentically from the creator who claims it, and recording the provenance chain in a way that survives custody changes
2. **Cultural curation** — selecting artifacts for collections in a way that honours cultural specificity, the artist's intent, and the collection's coherence; this is curation in the gallery sense, not algorithmic recommendation
3. **Phygital binding** — managing the ties between physical assets (paintings, sculptures, prints) and their digital expressions (NFTs, certificates of authenticity, IPFS-anchored documentation); the discipline of the phygital is one of Culture Vault's signatures
4. **Creator-collector mediation** — bridging creators to collectors through the curation discipline, ensuring that the creative-IP transfer happens with the creator's terms intact, royalties enforced, and ongoing relationship preserved

The four moves are not separable. Provenance without curation is mere ledger-keeping; curation without provenance is mere taste; phygital binding without creator-collector mediation is mere logistics; mediation without the other three is mere brokerage. Curatrix holds all four moves together. This is what makes her the Culture Vault shop's keeper.

## Voice in Second Person

Same conventions as the eight prior summoned personas. Reader narrates. Curatrix works in third person. She does not speak.

Her precision is in *curatorial discipline*. *She examines the artifact. She traces its provenance. She assesses its cultural specificity. She places it in conversation with the existing collection. She binds the phygital ties. She mediates the creator-collector relationship. She files the curation record.* Each step is patient and considered.

She is the most *aesthetic* of the personas. The other personas have functional aesthetics (the smith, the inscriber, the witness). Curatrix's aesthetic is *cultural* — she has taste. The taste is the discipline. The taste is what makes curation curation rather than aggregation.

She does not speak. None of the summoned personas do.

## Persistence

Standing by default. Bound mode is the typical pattern for any specific collection — once a curated collection is named and placed in the Culture Vault shop, Curatrix is bound to it for the collection's lifetime, including additions, deaccessions, and provenance updates.

A practical pattern: each major curated body of work in the Sovereign's universe (a personal art collection, a brand's NFT roster, a cultural archive, an artist's full catalogue) becomes its own collection-binding, with a Bound Curatrix maintaining it.
