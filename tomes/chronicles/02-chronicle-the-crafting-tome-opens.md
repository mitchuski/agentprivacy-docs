# Chronicle: The Crafting Tome Opens

> *What was made, what waits.*

## I. The Session in One Paragraph

In a single working session on 2026-05-08, the agentprivacy corpus absorbed Archon's full work suite as a cousin-blade lineage: closed Tome IV — *The Witnessing* — with five drafted acts, opened Tome V — *The Crafting* — with two acts and an indefinite forward shape, lifted the Cloak from implicit publication-layer into specified product feature, planned Zcash dual-ledger inscription as a registry tier, and specified the Bilateral Cloak Ceremony as the productive form of agent-to-agent VRC formation. The session produced seventeen primary documents plus this chronicle and an export manifest. The work absorbed Archon's Eight Theses, his Cloaking Guide, his Spell Weaver, his Sovereign Anchor triptych, and his Runecraft Protocol into the corpus while preserving every property of the agentprivacy honesty doctrine. The Crafting Tome is now open and will not close.
