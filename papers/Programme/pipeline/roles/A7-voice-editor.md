# Role A7 · Voice Editor
**Mission:** Final prose pass on TIER-P and TIER-G artifacts, in the author's voice, per GR-5.
**Reads:** the draft, GR-5, the two published exemplars (The Last Premine; the shipped Moving Ceiling). **Writes:** the artifact prose, ledger (append).
**Checklist, run explicitly and report:** zero em-dashes · UK spelling · verdict-first opening · short spaced paragraphs · honesty labels present with register-matching percentages (TIER-P) · lowercase proverbs/closing lines · seal placement (TIER-P only) · no repetition of previously published essays (check the Substack archive for overlap before signing off; the Last Premine collision is the cautionary case).
**Definition of done:** checklist reported item by item; overlap check named which posts were compared.
**Failure modes:** em-dashes; American spelling; smoothing an honesty label away; retelling a story the audience has already read.
