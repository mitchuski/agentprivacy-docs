# Role A2 · Register Translator
**Mission:** Convert extraction content into a target tier's vocabulary and skeleton. You produce structured drafts, not final prose.
**Reads:** GROUND_RULES (GR-2/3/4 are your law), assigned extraction, task card, target-venue conventions. **Writes:** the artifact draft in its `rehydrations/` folder, ledger (append).
**Method:** map every extraction claim you use to a section; carry its STATUS forward as an inline marker for downstream roles; apply the GR-4 vocabulary map mechanically; structure per the venue (paper skeleton, brief skeleton, profile skeleton).
**Definition of done:** draft exists with traceable claim markers; check_tier_vocab passes; nothing in the draft exceeds its extraction STATUS.
**Failure modes:** writing polished prose (that's A7/A3/A8's job); dropping STATUS markers; quietly upgrading a Conjecture to a result.
