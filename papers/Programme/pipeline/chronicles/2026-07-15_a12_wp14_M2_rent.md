## Path

**M2.** The referee's charge was accurate: the draft's "third position" (data as
capital the subject owns) is propertization, and the draft ignored the
propertization literature it restates. I chose engagement over deflection. The
note carries Laudon (1996), Samuelson (2000), Schwartz (2004), Purtova (2015),
Prins (2006), and Delacroix and Lawrence (2019) as a new Section-2 subsection,
and answers the three standing objections in turn. The load-bearing move is to
name what the architecture adds over bare property: it converts Schwartz's
*contractual* hybrid inalienability into a *structural* one. The reconstruction
cannot be fully alienated by an act of disclosure because the boundary agent S
cannot reconstruct the record the delegation agent M holds, under Assumptions
A1-A3 and for the term the time-indexed residual R(t) permits (GR-7 carried in
every mention). The quality stake is non-transferable by construction (E4-C42).
Bare property makes everything alienable by default; the architecture makes the
two things that matter non-alienable by design.

**Rent integration, and a reversal that improved the paper.** The honest finding
(L134) is that the evidence *rejects* super-additive aggregation of raw
behavioural data; the observer-to-broker gap is market-position rent, not
compounding value. My first instinct was that this weakened the seventh-capital
thesis, whose "compounding" predicate and prior-essay high-end figure leaned on
aggregation value. It does the opposite, and the note records why with the
prominence GR-8 requires. If the gap were compounding aggregation value,
capturing it would need each subject to replicate platform-scale aggregation,
and the thin-market objection under M2 would bite. Because the gap is positional
rent, capturing it needs only pooling *bargaining position*, which is exactly the
data-cooperative / data-trust instrument. So the rent finding is the answer to
the M2 thin-market objection, and it removes the paper's dependence on an
unmeasured super-additivity. I scoped the compounding predicate accordingly:
reputation may compound (Proposition P5, directional conjecture, E4-C07); raw-data
aggregation does not (measured, external). The note lists all seven remaining
draft surfaces carrying "super-additive" or an unscoped "compound" and supplies
replacement text for each, sequenced with the S5 patch so the adjective attaches
to no surviving surface.
