## Harness lessons banked this cycle

- Prior-art-before-drafting paid immediately: the draft's novelty
  section was written inside a fence instead of negotiating one after
  a reviewer objects (the WP-04 L062 lesson, now standard).
- An extraction's honest depth statement is a working fence: the
  widening's "survey-plus-sample" and "method-depth" labels let the
  draft consume the material without overclaiming coverage.
- The method's failures are its best evidence: the 111/111
  contradiction, found by the method's own fourth-generation
  instrument, became the paper's strongest operating-evidence passage
  rather than a liability.
- Formalisation discipline has a direction too: define what the design
  enforces by construction; state what operation showed as instance
  evidence; never let the first borrow the second's grade.
