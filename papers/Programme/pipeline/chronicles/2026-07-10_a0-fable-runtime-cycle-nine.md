## Harness lessons banked this cycle

- Read the checks before drafting, not after: both new artifacts (E7,
  WP-09) passed all four checks on first invocation because their seats
  studied the hazard classes first.
- A genre is a fence boundary: the same tier admits different claim
  classes in a policy brief vs a discussion paper; rule it explicitly
  rather than letting the strictest prior instance govern silently.
- The ledger-tail audit is cheap and pays: one orphan in sixteen
  entries, found the first time anyone looked systematically.
- Extraction builds are corpus audits in disguise: E7's real find was a
  GR-7 breach on a canon-adjacent surface nobody had swept.
