# The Twelve Cycles

*a synthesis chronicle of the Fable auto-research runtime, written for the first person's reading*

---

## Verdict, first

In four calendar days the runtime took a research programme that had one
policy brief in flight and turned it into five artifacts standing at their
hard stops: two academic papers aimed at PoPETs and the FC workshops, a
methods paper that survived its own method, a grant edition with eight
fundable work packages, and a standards paper addressed to a working group
by its verified name. It built five extractions, hardened its own checking
tools twice, absorbed the first external observer audit in the programme's
history, verified that observer's sharpest mathematical criticism, and then
carried the correction into the canon that is about to be socialised — with
every edit backed up, every change traced, and the one gate that matters
never once crossed by a machine.

The register head did not move. Twelve cycles, sixty-seven ledger entries,
roughly forty agent seats, and not one conjecture was minted, promoted, or
renumbered by the runtime. That is not an accident of restraint; it is the
design working. The fleet's job was never to decide what is true. Its job
was to drive every artifact to the exact line where only your signature
can take it further, and to leave a record honest enough that you can sign
without re-deriving the work.

Everything below is that record, told as one story.

## Prologue: what was standing when the runtime booted

The runtime did not start from nothing. The pipeline itself — GROUND_RULES,
the role cards A0 through A13, the manifest, the append-only critiques
ledger, the four deterministic checks — was built in the fleet days of
early July, and by the evening of 2026-07-03 it had already run three
cycles in one day, executed a canon-level correction (the L044
decomposition, which taught the whole programme the difference between what
the preconditions buy and what must be separately declared), stripped the
canon of its fiat figures, and stood up the tracker so the work could be
watched. Two extractions existed: E1, the amnesia-gap claims, and E2, the
moving ceiling. One artifact, the policy brief, had fought through two
adversarial reviews to release-draft-v6.

What the Fable runtime added on 2026-07-07 was a standing pattern: the
elevation loop. Every paper runs the same stations — prior-art scout,
formal draft, citation verification, adversarial review in a venue-true
persona, deterministic consistency audit — one role per session, every
session ending in a chronicle, every finding filed to a ledger that cannot
be rewritten. And a hard stop, stated in the boot note and never once
softened: the runtime stops at P3. P4 — the completion read, the
submission decision, the signature — is yours and cannot be simulated,
summarised, or assumed.

## Movement I · the enablers and the first proofs (cycles 1-3, 2026-07-07)

Cycle 1 did the unglamorous things that every later cycle stood on. A13
verified every venue deadline at its primary page — and the verification
mattered immediately: PoPETs Issue 2 turned out to close 2026-08-31, BGIN
pulled forward to October, First Monday was closed entirely. A8 built the
LaTeX submission scaffold and compiled it. A10 swept WP-04's prior art
before a single sentence of the paper existed, forty works verified,
novelty fenced — the discipline that would later be named the L062 rule:
novelty established before asserted.

Cycle 2 produced the arc's first mathematics. A3 took the linear-cap paper
and proved the Nε bound as a real theorem — conditional on five explicit
erasure requirements, with necessity shown in both directions and a
tightness construction — leaving exactly one conjecture riding as a
conjecture because its missing step could be named. The SoK got its
draft the same day, fifteen claims from E2, every one carrying its trace
marker. The policy brief's second review found a self-contradiction — the
reviewer's own first-review prescription had gone stale under an approved
reframe — and the loop closed it the same day.

Cycle 3 taught the runtime how to correct its own upstream. The register
brief pattern emerged: extraction edits execute pipeline-side, canon
changes get filed as a brief for your ruling, and nothing bleeds across
that line. WP-04 passed its extraction-fidelity gate; WP-07 got its
citation station, where A4 found the paper importing a theorem with more
strength than its source carried — the import-fidelity defect class that
every later citation station hunted by name.

## Movement II · two papers reach the stop (cycles 4-5, 2026-07-08/09)

Cycle 4 was the first end-to-end ladder. WP-07 went from completion draft
through the crypto-PC review — MAJOR REVISION, one BLOCKING: the reviewer
constructed a counterexample to Proposition 4.2 under carried state, and
the proposition was genuinely false as stated. This is the moment the
adversarial station earned its cost. The same-day loop repaired the
mathematics honestly — the reviewer's own construction was adopted into
the paper as Proposition 5.5b rather than argued around — and the targeted
re-check against the reviewer's own successor criterion stood in for a
second full review. Then the consistency station blocked on a single
pre-review sentence, a micro-touch fixed it, and WP-07 became the first
paper at awaiting-P4.

Cycle 5 did it again in one day. WP-04's citations came back 44/44; the
SoK review filed four MAJORs with the core clean; the revision loop
resolved them the honest way (a definition's bridge demoted to a
desideratum rather than strengthened against an unexhibited witness); and
the one unread neighbour — Kagai et al., behind a publisher's 403 wall —
was read referee-grade via the asset-host route and engaged in the text
rather than left as a risk. Two papers at the stop. The same cycle, almost
in passing, produced the dual-agent harness brief and registered your
circuit-workshop note as the trust-gated loop's second instance — the seed
of everything Movement IV became.

Cycle 4 also banked the runtime's most consequential tooling lesson: the
checks were passing vacuously when invoked without arguments. Green
evidence that had checked nothing. It was caught, corrected on the record,
and became first a hardening item, then a guard, then — much later — the
exact war story an outside observer's audit would independently validate
as the non-vacuous-checker rule.

## Movement III · the maintenance cycle that refused to be small (cycle 6, 2026-07-09)

Cycle 6 was cut as housekeeping: close the three standing hardening items,

It closed all of that — the argv-less guard now fails loudly, the

The lesson that cycle banked has held ever since: programme-shape defects
are ruling material, not prose material. A criterion that cannot fail is
not fixed by better wording; it is fixed by someone with authority
deciding what failure means.

## What the runtime learned about itself

The lessons are in the cycle chronicles individually; these are the ones
that now function as standing law.

**The reviewer's successor criterion replaces the second review.** Five
times a MAJOR revision closed with a targeted re-check against
conditions the reviewer wrote down. The re-check is cheaper than a
review and stricter than one, because the criterion was authored by the
adversary at maximum knowledge.

**Fix known findings before the reviewer reads.** Every pre-review
repair is a finding the review does not spend attention on. Reviewer
attention goes where the repairs are not — which is what you want.

**Tier discipline and venue fitness are orthogonal.** A document can be
flawless against the rulebook and dead in the room. The personas carry
the venue's rubric, not just the tier's.

**A hardening fix is done when a different seat fails to break it.** The
A0-then-A9 double verification pattern cost one extra station each time
and closed every item permanently.

**Extraction builds are audits.** Four builds, four finds: a phantom
registry row, a canon-adjacent GR-7 breach, a register-drifted practice
record, an instrument's own overclaim. Reading the corpus claim-by-claim
under trace-or-delete is the deepest sweep the programme has.

**The fence within the fix.** The hardest part of correcting "holds
exactly when" everywhere was not touching the one "holds exactly when"
that is genuinely an iff. Precision cuts both ways, and both seats held
it.

**State current fact; reserve the promise.** Whenever an artifact needed
to speak about something only you can commit to — record availability,
document availability — the runtime wrote the fact and reserved the
promise, in-line, with the release checklist carrying the item. That
pattern is now used three times and has never minted an obligation on
your behalf.

**Nothing-on-disk means relaunch, not resume.** The resume discipline
protects completed work; it does not apply to a seat that died at boot.
Verify the disk first; the disk decides.
