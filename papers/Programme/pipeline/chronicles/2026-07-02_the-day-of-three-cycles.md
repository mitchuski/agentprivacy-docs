# The day of three cycles

## Verdict

On 2026-07-02 the V6 Rehydration Pipeline went from an empty task directory to a proven working method in three cycles and seventeen sessions. It built its first extraction, carried its first artifact through the full producer and review chain to release-draft v5, shipped forty-three ledger entries, and, at the day's centre, did the one thing that justifies its existence: an adversarial reviewer reading with no canon access and no goodwill found the research programme's central guarantee stated too strongly, the finding survived tracing through the extraction into the specification itself, and by the day's end the canon, the extractions and the downstream artifact all carried the corrected mathematics, with every edit recorded for first-person review and nothing resolved from a seat that did not own it. The pipeline's premise, that structured adversarial roles surface what a single author cannot, is no longer a premise.

## Where the day started

The morning's state: manifest v3 at register head C96, one built extraction (E2), no task cards beyond two exemplars, the gathering arc's three essays freshly coherence-checked, and a first-cycle plan in the README that had never been run. The first person said: start the fleet.

## Cycle one: the producers

Four sessions in parallel. A1 built E1, the amnesia-gap extraction, thirty-nine claims at head C96 with no contested items, unblocking three work packages at once. A2 built the policy brief's TIER-S skeleton a month ahead of the programme's only hard external date. A9 discharged the whitepaper hygiene pass that had blocked WP-03 since L003, finding the document disagreeing with itself about its own version three ways and six static-ceiling passages standing unconditioned; it also caught the check suite silently false-passing on its retired-citation rule. A7 ran the voice and overlap pass on the arc's third essay and, by fetching the published Last Premine live, closed the long-open question of the quoted closing lines (present, verbatim) and the phrase "Ironwood manoeuvre" (absent; the image is real, the proper noun was a coinage dressed as a quotation).

Two protocol adaptations were made in the open: ledger appends were serialised through A0 because append-only does not survive four concurrent writers on one file, and the branch convention was suspended because the tree is untracked and the standing rule forbids commits without an explicit ask.

## Cycle two: the reviewers

Seven sessions, staged so no two held the same file. The skeleton was re-traced against the built E1, deleting one sentence whose only home was barred at TIER-S rather than defending it. WP-02 passed P0 under A0's own spot-trace. The check-script defect was repaired with a regression proof, and the repair surfaced a design question it declined to bury: extractions fail the versions check precisely because they quote the canon faithfully. E2 was re-swept to head C96, catching a wording drift that had quietly tightened C81's Stage-2 bar with a domain qualifier the register never carried, and admitting three Band IX claims. A4 verified every citation in both arc essays against primary sources with zero edits required, and closed the two standing liveness questions: the guide is live, and the atlas publicly serves the leakage-fold instrument exactly as the essay describes it, register-bound, down to the 31-epsilon-versus-5-epsilon arithmetic. A8 expanded the brief to release shape against two fetched exemplars, the EDPB-EDPS Joint Opinion 1/2026 and a Bruegel policy brief.

Then A5, persona regulator, reading the artifact alone: verdict MAJOR, one BLOCKING item. The brief asserted R_max = (C_S + C_M)/H(X) < 1 as a consequence of its two architectural preconditions, non-collusion and a stated adversary class. Neither precondition constrains the capacities relative to H(X). The correction session confirmed the worst and best case simultaneously: the conflation was carried verbatim in extraction E2-C01 and sourced to spec §10 and §11. The artifact was fixed same-day to the decomposition; the canon wording was escalated CANON-LEVEL, not resolved locally. The register process, which is to say the first person, now owned it.

## The decisions

At the close of cycle two the first person directed the decisions be made. Four rulings, recorded at L045: the canon corrections batch approved at full rigour, in-passage GR-7 conditioning ruled required and the blanket edition note ruled insufficient; the Ironwood line reworded to own its coinage; the division-of-authority clause trimmed per the arc's ownership table; the fold instrument's honesty label hardened to "proven as a bound and evidenced empirically in the literature", and the guide's fourth-door line kept as stated aspiration over the deployed snapshot. Every ruling revertible at the P4 read.

## Cycle three: the canon executes

Six sessions. Backups of all three canon files were taken before any edit. The specification's central guarantee was decomposed at ten passages across the formal and summary specs, including a new capacity-deficit paragraph placed deliberately beside the preconditions and deliberately not among them: preconditions are architectural and attestable; the deficit is numerical and time-eroding; what expires at t* is the deficit condition, not the architecture. The whitepaper's eight-item batch was executed, check_versions falling from eight findings to one, and the document's strongest sentence rewritten to earn its rhetoric: "60 pieces remain forever unreconstructable" became "stay beyond that adversary's reach for as long as that capacity deficit holds", and "Sovereignty lives in that permanent gap" became "Sovereignty lives in that gap, for as long as the deficit condition holds", which is truer to the model than the original was.

The extractions followed the canon the same day: E2-C01 re-issued and E1-C02 tightened, quote-traced to the corrected file rather than to any intermediary; E1's filter note reconciled with its FEEDS lines on the stricter reading; C91, which the morning's sweep had found homeless, extracted as E1-C40. The A5 re-check returned blocking-resolved with no new BLOCKING, WP-02 passed P1, and the combined A2+A8 revision resolved all four MAJOR review items at release-draft v5: the legal bridge rerouted through GDPR Articles 25 and 32 with Article 22 recast as the rights outcome and an explicit non-overclaim disclaimer; every recommendation naming who acts and through which instrument; an attestation subsection stating in terms that the declared quantities are today declarations rather than measurements; and the policy-versus-architecture dichotomy reframed as three complementary layers distinguished by failure mode. Nothing legal was written from memory; every article was verified against the published text.

## What the day surfaced that nobody was looking for

- The specification stated its central guarantee too strongly, and had for months (L044; found by an artifact review, not a canon audit).
- E2 had quietly tightened C81's Stage-2 bar beyond the register's wording (L036).
- C91 had no extraction home anywhere (L034); the limitative note's ceiling join carries no register number at all (L035).
- The VRC protocol document self-identifies as version 3.4 under a v3_3 filename, so the whitepaper's supposedly wrong citation was faithful to the document and unfaithful to the registry (L049).
- The t* supremum needs monotonicity or a first-crossing definition, and E2-C04 already holds the formalisation (L054).
- eprint 2026/1128 is single-authored; no canon surface says otherwise, but the pipeline's own briefings did (L039).
- The check suite had two blind spots of its own (L024, L055(c)), both found by running it against real canon rather than trusting it.

## Reversals, standing

The deficit condition was nearly named Precondition 3 and deliberately was not. The essays' P2 evidence was gathered but their gate held at P0 rather than being advanced past an unrun P1. The spec executor declined an out-of-batch fix it could see; the revision session declined to alter a definition from a rehydration seat. The day's discipline was as much in what was not done as in what was.

## Where the day ended

- The gathering arc: WP-01 draft v4 and WP-25 draft v2, citation-verified, liveness-confirmed, wording resolved; waiting on nothing but the first person's P4 reads, in that order. WP-28 draft v3 behind them on the arc schedule.

- The canon: corrected at both specs and the whitepaper, pending the first-person diff review that is the batch's completion condition (before/after records in the two canon chronicles; backups intact; git diff available).
- The extractions: E1 at forty claims, E2 at fifteen, both conformant to the corrected canon.
- The ledger: L055, with the open items owned and the resolved items closed by reference, never by rewrite.

the pipeline was built on the bet that honesty scales better than confidence. on its first full day, the bet paid in both directions: the walls held, and the thing they caught was ours.
