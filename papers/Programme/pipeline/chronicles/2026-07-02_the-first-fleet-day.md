# The first fleet day

## Verdict

On 2026-07-02 the V6 Rehydration Pipeline ran its first two production cycles: eleven single-role sessions under one standing A0, thirty-three ledger entries (L013 to L045), and every artifact it touched left better than it arrived. The day's proof of concept is not the volume but a single finding's trajectory: a hostile reviewer with no canon access caught the central guarantee of the policy brief overstated, the correction traced the overstatement through the extraction into the specification itself, and the machinery did exactly what it was built to do, fixing the downstream artifact same-day while escalating the canon wording to the register process instead of resolving it quietly (L042 to L044). The pipeline's premise, that structured adversarial roles surface what a single author cannot, held on first contact.

## Cycle one: the producers

Four parallel sessions, launched on the README's first-cycle plan. A1 built E1, the amnesia-gap extraction, complete at thirty-nine claims against register head C96 with no contested items; it is the inventory WP-02, WP-07 and WP-09 draw from. A2 built the policy brief's TIER-S skeleton a month ahead of the only hard external date (2026-08-02), on the fast-pass fallback since E1 was landing in the same cycle. A9 discharged the whitepaper hygiene pass that had blocked WP-03 since L003, filing nine findings spanning the document's three-way disagreement about its own version, retired citations, six unconditioned static-ceiling passages, and the figure fence; it also caught the check suite itself false-passing on the retired-citation rule. A7 ran the voice and overlap pass on the arc's third essay, cutting four overlaps per the arc's ownership table, and, by fetching the published Last Premine live, closed the long-open L011(g): the quoted closing lines are in the published text verbatim; the phrase "Ironwood manoeuvre" is not.

Two protocol adaptations were made deliberately and recorded: the append-only ledger cannot survive four concurrent writers on one file, so producers returned proposed entries and A0 serialised them; and the branch convention was suspended because the tree is untracked and the standing rule forbids commits without an explicit ask.

## Cycle two: the reviewers

Seven sessions, staged rather than fully parallel so that no two sessions held the same file. A2 re-traced the skeleton against the built E1, deleting one sentence whose only extraction home was barred at TIER-S (trace or delete, never defend). A4 verified every citation in both arc essays against primary sources, zero edits required, and closed the two standing liveness questions: the guide is live, and the atlas publicly serves the leakage-fold instrument exactly as the essay describes it, register-bound at head C96. A8 expanded the brief to release shape against two fetched exemplars, the EDPB-EDPS Joint Opinion 1/2026 and a Bruegel policy brief. Then A5, reading the artifact alone with no goodwill, returned MAJOR with one BLOCKING item.

## The finding of the day

A5's blocking finding: the brief asserted R_max = (C_S + C_M)/H(X) < 1 as a consequence of its two architectural preconditions, non-collusion and a fixed adversary class. Neither precondition constrains the capacities relative to H(X); the strict bound is an additional, measurable, system-specific condition. The correction session confirmed the conflation is carried verbatim in extraction E2-C01 and is sourced to spec §10 and §11, so the specification presumably states its central guarantee too strongly (L044, CANON-LEVEL, register process). The brief was restated at draft v4: the preconditions buy the additive structure and the error floor; the strict bound waits on the declared capacity deficit C_S + C_M < H(X); and the deficit was deliberately not named a third precondition, because preconditions are architectural and attestable while the deficit is numerical and time-eroding. The restatement made the brief stronger in its own terms: what expires at t* is the deficit condition, not the architecture.
