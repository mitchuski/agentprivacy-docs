## The mathematical findings, in order of importance

1. **The load-bearing distinction is not erasure alone; it is adversary scope plus budget form.** The imported sequential bound governs the final-output adversary. A two-agent construction (Proposition 4.3: O_1 = S_1 XOR N_1, O_2 = Z_1 XOR S_2) satisfies erasure and zero marginal budgets, including interface-inclusive marginals, yet surrenders a context completely to the transcript adversary, the adversary the AgentLeak measurements say is the real one. The attribute that prices the attack is conditioning on the received interface. This sharpens the extraction's reading of E1-c09/c10 and should interest the register process (proposal 3 below).

2. **Erasure is what makes the received interface a sufficient statistic of the past.** The proof of Theorem 5.1 fails at exactly one step when carried state survives, and the one-time-pad construction of Proposition 5.5 (V_1 = X XOR rho, V_2 = rho, both budgets zero, joint leakage H(X)) realises the failure. Necessity and sufficiency are therefore both exhibited, which is the strongest honest form of the claim that the chain break is structural.

## Reversals and dead ends (recorded with equal prominence)

- **Re-deriving the doubling failed, and the failure is recorded rather than papered over.** I attempted to close the recursion L_i <= 2 L_{i-1} + epsilon_i from marginal per-agent constraints alone inside the model. It does not close: with exogenous contexts the same computation yields the additive recursion even without erasure (for the final-output adversary), so the doubling must come from context contamination, and the general contamination term I(O_{i-1}; S_i | S_{1:i-1}) admits no bound in terms of L_{i-1} without an added hypothesis. Proposition 4.2 therefore states the reproduction under a named sufficient condition (bounded contamination), and the artifact says in a binding scope note that correspondence with the source paper's hypotheses is an open citation-resolution item for A4. The imported theorem rests on its citation, not on my reproduction.
- **Additivity is an inequality.** I began by following E1-c04's "additive" equality phrasing and reversed to the exact form: I(X; Y_S, Y_M) <= I(X; Y_S) + I(X; Y_M) under conditional independence, with equality iff additionally marginal independence holds. The v4.3 body itself carries this correction in its critical note; the extraction's usage (the bound direction) is unaffected. No canon conflict; a precision restatement.
- **The normalised Fano gloss needs a hypothesis.** The form P_e >= 1 − R − 1/H(X) requires H(X) >= log(|X_alphabet| − 1) (near-uniformity), which the lineage gloss elides via "large alphabets". Theorem 3.2 states the exact form and the hypothesis. Hygiene record only (proposal 5).
