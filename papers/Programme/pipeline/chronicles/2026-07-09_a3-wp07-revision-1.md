After: "the same computation yields the additive recursion L_i ≤ L_{i−1} + ε_i under the same no-carried-state hypothesis, *for the final-output adversary*." **Hand verification of exclusion.** The reviewer's counterexample retains ρ_1 = S_1 across the boundary; under (i) the composition must satisfy O_2 = f_2(O_1, S_2, N_2), and S_1 reaches invocation 2 only through O_1 = U, which is independent junk, so the construction is unrepresentable.
