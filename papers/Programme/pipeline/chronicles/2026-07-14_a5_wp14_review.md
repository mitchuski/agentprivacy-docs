## What I attacked, and what held

Attacked the weakest links first, per the task's five tests.

1. **Positioned inside the data-markets literature?** Partly. Section 2 is a
   genuine three-literature placement (Acquisti; data-as-labour; Spiekermann/
   Akerlof lemons) and states the data-as-labour tension rather than eliding it.
   But the claimed-novel "third position" (data as subject-owned capital) is
   propertization, and the paper ignores the decades-old, largely critical
   personal-data-as-property literature (Laudon, Samuelson, Schwartz, Purtova,
   Prins) that occupies exactly that position. **MAJOR-2.** The subject-side
   aggregator is the MID/data-cooperative instrument, under-attributed (MINOR m7).

2. **Every value claim names a mechanism and carries ranges?** Mostly yes, and
   the market mechanism is named and load-bearing (Section 5.3). But the draft
   dropped the A12-note numeric ranges and kept the "roughly one order of
   magnitude" share-shift conclusion in prose with no interval on either
   endpoint — a smuggled semi-quantitative residue of the retired 678x, and a
   rigor regression relative to the paper's own substrate. **MAJOR-4.** No GR-3
   figure breach: 678x/31,000x/70:1/74x/fiat appear nowhere.

3. **Reconstruction ceiling without preconditions + R(t)?** No breach — the
   opposite. GR-7 handled with unusual care: A1-A3 numbered, reused by
   reference, and P4 explicitly quarantines the source's retired "guarantees
   collapse" sentence and refuses to import it. Logged as a strength. The
   over-reach that survives is economic, not GR-7: the "term structure" claim is
   drawn from the existence of R(t) without its schedule. **MAJOR-3.**

4. **Over-claim beyond proven-conditional; conjectures honestly labelled?**
   Conjectures are labelled at or below extraction status (C55 as thesis-with-
   obligation, C42/C95/E4-C07 as conjectures, M(u,y) as proposal, multipliers as
   illustrative). GR-8 reporting-commitment present. The over-claim that remains
   is subtler: the Results section states propositions whose derivations are all
   deferred as "proof obligations" — honest about not having done the work, but
   the work is still not done. **MAJOR-1** (contribution).

5. **Novel and interdisciplinary-legible?** Legible: yes. Novel: the synthesis
   is interesting but the load-bearing technical novelty is imported from WP-07
   (unavailable to the referee, MAJOR-3), and the "seventh capital" ordinal
   indexes off an accounting-reporting frame arbitrary to economists
   (**MAJOR-5**).
