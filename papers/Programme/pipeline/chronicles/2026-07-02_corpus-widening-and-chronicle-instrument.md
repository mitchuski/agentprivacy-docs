# Corpus widening and the chronicle instrument

**Produced this session:** the chronicles directory and its convention (this folder); a five-way inventory sweep of the full corpus, launched and pending; the decision that extraction source lists E1 to E9 must be widened before the production fleet is spawned.

## The finding that opened the session

The programme document (v2) and manifest name their sources almost entirely as formal-spec sections, whitepaper sections, and the conjecture register. The wider body of work is not named: the living documentation in agentprivacy-docs (tomes, chronicles, models, research notes), the cityofmages canon and its grimoire lineage, the canonical skills corpus (an operational practice of the model at 137+ units), the soulbis site's City Key and lattice implementations, the zk blades forge (circuits, UOR mappings, key-ceremony specifications), and the reference implementations spread across sibling repositories. Under GR-9 (trace or delete), an unnamed source is an unusable source: any claim that lives only in the unnamed corpus can never legitimately reach a rehydration.

The owner's framing, recorded as programme intent: the body of work maps the path as well as the outcomes, and the path is a discovery in itself. The first rehydration of fundamentals in many lights follows the concept that privacy is value. Coverage must therefore include the process record, not only the results.

## Decisions

1. **Widen at the source layer, not the artifact layer.** The fix is to extend the `sources:` lists of E1 to E9 and add extractions where the work fits none, rather than to loosen tracing discipline downstream.
2. **The path becomes an extraction.** The register discipline, the gates, the two-register method, and the chronicle record will be swept into a dedicated extraction feeding the methods chapter (thesis Ch.7) and any methods-venue artifact. This chronicles directory is created partly to be that extraction's living source.
3. **Formal register locked for pipeline instruments.** Pipeline-facing documents, including chronicles, use GR-4 vocabulary; canon vocabulary stays upstream in the canon.
4. **Inventory before synthesis.** Five parallel inventory agents sweep: (a) agentprivacy-docs excluding the pipeline itself; (b) cityofmages; (c) agentprivacy_master plus sibling working directories (spellweb, star, game42, shor_mage, privacymage_book, myterms bundle, guide snapshot); (d) agentprivacy-skills-v5 and the soulbis site; (e) zk blades forge and the swordsman-blade formal documents. Output: a coverage matrix keyed to E1 to E9 plus proposed new extractions, then manifest deltas.

## Handoff

- **Open:** inventory agents pending; synthesis of the source registry, manifest deltas, and instruction updates follows their return.
- **Blocked:** production fleet spawn is deliberately held until source coverage is closed, so first drafts trace against the full corpus rather than a partial one.
- **Next action:** on inventory return, write the corpus source registry, apply widened `sources:` lists and new extraction stubs to the manifest, and update the boot documents (CLAUDE.md, README) to bind the chronicle convention on every session.
