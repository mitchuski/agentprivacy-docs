One reversal to record. The first drafting instinct was to add the capacity deficit as a third precondition in the indented list. Reversed before writing: the two preconditions are architectural and declarative (properties the deployment pattern enforces by construction), while the deficit is a numerical, system-specific, time-eroding fact that must be measured. Labelling it a precondition would have re-imported the conflation in different clothing, suggesting the architecture supplies it. It is now set in the same indented block but named as a condition of a different kind, "measurable and declarable", and section 3 identifies it as the quantity that expires at t* ("what expires at t* is the deficit condition, not the architecture").
