research-council-class), reading `rehydrations/grants/grant_edition.md`

allergic to advocacy dressed as research; rewards falsifiability, costed
deliverables, and honest limits; punishes claims that outrun evidence,
unmeasurable success criteria, and any smell of a foregone conclusion.

**VERDICT: MAJOR REVISION.** 0 BLOCKING, 4 MAJOR, 5 MINOR. Funder terms: the proposition is fundable and, after revision, competitive in a twenty-proposal field; as submitted, two of the eight evaluation criteria are unmeasurable in place, the "each independently fundable" claim does not survive inspection, and one package's data plan has no feasibility statement. Those are exactly the defect classes an assessor is briefed to punish, and they sit inside a document whose pitch is that its evaluation discipline is the differentiator. This review is NOT P1 evidence; routing and the successor criterion are at the end.

**Summary (3 sentences).** The document converts one conditional theorem family plus external field measurements into eight work packages, holding the proven core to its full decomposition (preconditions, declared capacity-deficit condition, time index) in every load-bearing passage, with no static-ceiling drift, no canonical or fiat figures, and implementation status reported per component. The honesty apparatus is genuinely above grant norm: T3 is carried as structure only and says so twice, negative outcomes are named as deliverables in most packages, and section 6's limits restate the body at equal or weaker strength. The defects are concentrated in section 4's evaluation criteria: WP-H cannot fail as written, WP-C's null trigger is unevaluable, the packages' declared independence is contradicted by two unstated dependencies, and WP-G presupposes a data source the document never establishes.

### Strengths

1. **The proven-core paragraph is stated honestly, not persuasively.** Section 2 (:107) carries both preconditions, states that they "do not by themselves place R_max below one", declares the deficit condition as a numerical fact about a system rather than a consequence of the architecture, and explains why the deficit is deliberately not a third precondition; the executive summary (:87) and Limits (:234) carry the same decomposition at equal or weaker strength, time-indexed in-passage. I checked every strict-bound passage against the L044 decomposition wording of E2-C01/E1-C02: no drift found.
2. **Most packages name discriminating outcomes with negative results as deliverables.** WP-A's "succeeds if the measurement discriminates, whichever way it falls" (:148), WP-B's independent-reproduction test (:158), WP-D's "including λ indistinguishable from zero ... retires the countermeasure hypothesis" (:178), and WP-G's "fits or is rejected on stated statistical criteria" (:208) are operational falsifiability, not ornament; WP-D is the model the weaker criteria should be rewritten to.
3. **Scope honesty a funder can audit.** The two-instance base is explicitly called insufficient for a law (:236), the drift reading is stated as the hypothesis WP-C tests rather than a premise (:125), the field's measurements are credited as the field's (:238), and specified-but-not-built is never presented as built (:192, :240).

### Findings

**MAJOR-1 (WP-H's evaluation criterion cannot fail).** Anchor: :218, "partial mechanisation is publishable and counts", against the deliverable at :214 and the section-4 verdict at :129 ("each names ... what a negative result looks like"). No floor is set on "partial", so every outcome of the mechanisation strand constitutes success and the package cannot fail; the obstruction strand has its bounded attempt and stop condition (:216) and is fine, but the criterion as a whole discriminates nothing, which contradicts the document's own advertised standard. A package that cannot fail is unfundable on this assessor's rubric regardless of merit. *What satisfies:* state a minimum mechanisation target below which the package reports failure (the natural floor: the proven core of section 2 mechanised end to end with its axiom list published), with partiality counting only above that floor; the obstruction branch needs no change.

**MAJOR-2 (WP-C's null trigger is unevaluable: no qualification rule, no window length).** Anchors: :168 ("two consecutive windows with no qualifying instance"), method :166, duration row :135. "Qualifying instance" is never defined (the WP-00 verification discipline at :164 governs verification of an instance, not what qualifies one as an instance of drift), and no window length is stated; if windows are annual, per the method's "annual public re-estimate", the named negative outcome cannot fire inside the 12-month funded period at all. The criterion's discriminating power therefore rests on an unstated rule under the programme's own control. This finding is about the criterion's evaluability, not about the indicative duration, which is fenced. *What satisfies:* define the qualification rule and the window length in the criterion, chosen so the null trigger can fire within the funded period or explicitly tied to the renewal decision; apply the same qualification rule to WP-E's "second qualifying instance" clause (:188).

**MAJOR-3 (the "independently fundable" claim is contradicted by two unstated dependencies).** Anchors: :91 and :99 ("each independently fundable"; "Each is written to be fundable alone") against :146 and :192, and :164 against :152. First, WP-A's erasure-separated arm requires an erasure-enforced implementation at chain depths 2 to 5, while the document itself states the strongest specified design has no code and the held-apart pattern has exactly one workflow-level instance; which artefact instantiates the arm is never said. Second, WP-C's deliverable of dated t* re-estimates requires precisely the capacity-evaluation methodology WP-B exists to create, and WP-B's own gap statement (:152, "declared capacities cannot be independently evaluated" without the formal model) undercuts WP-C standing alone. An assessor funding WP-A or WP-C singly, as invited, buys an unstated dependency. *What satisfies:* name WP-A's erasure-arm instantiation from the built record (the deployed browser-storage design or the workflow harness, with its depth capability) or state the WP-F dependency; state the WP-C-on-WP-B interface or the interim methodology WP-C uses if funded alone; qualify the independence sentence accordingly.

**MAJOR-4 (WP-G's data plan has no feasibility statement).** Anchors: :206 ("instrument an operating credential network"), deliverable :204, duration row :139. The method presupposes an operating relationship-credential network whose existence, access, and instrumentability the document nowhere states, and proposes fitting decay curves in a 12-month longitudinal window for half-lives the programme has not bounded even to order of magnitude; a longitudinal package with an unnamed data source and an unargued observation window is a standard assessor kill. This is an artifact defect, not a project criticism: the grant text must let the funder assess data feasibility, and it does not. *What satisfies:* name the network (or state the build dependency and its implementation status per the per-component discipline of :226), and add one feasibility sentence covering expected edge volume, observation cadence, and the named reportable outcome if the window proves too short to bound the decay constants.

**MINOR-1 (the executive summary strengthens the Zcash record beyond the verified account).** Anchor: :89, "until a newly released model found it within a day of release", against :123, "found ... by an auditor using a model released the previous day", and E2-C05. The summary attributes the find to the model where the verified record attributes it to an auditor using the model; the strengthening sits in the document's most-read paragraph and flatters the capability-arrival narrative the funding case rests on. *What satisfies:* one clause, for example "until an auditor using a model released the previous day found it within a day of that release".

**MINOR-2 (E2-C14 rendered with the model requirement attached to the wrong component).** Anchor: :182, "monotone non-increasing in the number of corroborating systems, which requires a fixed corroboration model to be stated cleanly", against the extraction's "monotonicity clean; the convex profile requires a fixed corroboration model" (E2-C14), and the trace map's "wording held" implication at :274. The draft is conservative in direction (it demands more before asserting less) but diverges from its extraction on which component needs the corroboration model, and the method line (:186) then fixes the model before evaluating a statement the extraction holds clean. Claim-level disagreement, so it is recorded here for the ledger rather than edited. *What satisfies:* reword to attach the fixed-model requirement to the steep-then-shallow profile (which the draft may continue to omit) and let the monotonicity statement stand as the extraction carries it; ledger entry either way.

**MINOR-3 (a reference no passage consumes, tagged to the wrong extraction claim).** Anchor: :261, "Bergstra, J. and Burgess, M. (2019) ... [grounding family context per E1-C05 citation strategy; no claim carried]". E1-C05's grounding family is Wyner, Fano, Leung-Yan-Cheong and Hellman, the QIF Bayes-capacity line, and Geiger and Kubin; Promise Theory belongs to E1-C17, which the trace map (:277) says is deliberately not consumed. **MINOR-4 (WP-A's criterion lacks a decision rule its siblings have).** Anchor: :148, "curves discriminate between the additive and sequential bounds in each arm", against WP-B's "within stated tolerance" (:158) and WP-G's "on stated statistical criteria" (:208). At N = 2 the two bounds differ by a single ε, so discrimination is a statistical-power question the criterion never surfaces; "discriminates" without a rule leaves success adjudicable by the programme. *What satisfies:* add the decision rule to the criterion (pre-registered test and effect threshold, and the depth range at which discrimination is powered).

**MINOR-5 (the pre-registration and public-register commitments carry no locator).** Anchors: :91 ("filed to a public register"), :226 ("pre-registered"; "the programme's public conjecture register"), against :262, where the public specification does get a URL.
