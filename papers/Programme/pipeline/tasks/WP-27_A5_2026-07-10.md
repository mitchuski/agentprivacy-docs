### Findings

**MAJOR-1 (the 111/111 catcher is misattributed; probe item 4).** Anchor:
:310, "the method's fourth-generation instrument (trace-or-delete extraction
under audit) is what caught the first generation's overclaim [E10-C24]".
E10-C24 fixes generation four as "deterministic checks proven non-vacuous,
with argv-less invocation exiting 2 by design" and attributes the catch to
the extraction sweep ("stood unnoticed in the corpus until this sweep").
Trace-or-delete extraction is a different instrument family (E10-C04); it is
not a generation of the coherence instrument the claim enumerates. The
sentence manufactures a closed gen-four-catches-gen-one arc the extraction
does not carry, and it is an instance of the paper's own named failure mode
(:178) firing inside the methods paper; the trace map's "nothing exceeds
its extraction STATUS" line (:385) is false while it stands. *What
satisfies:* reword so that "generation four" keeps the extraction's
denotation (the deterministic non-vacuous checks) and the 111/111 catch is
attributed to the extraction sweep operating under the trace-or-delete
discipline; any wording checked verbatim against E10-C24 suffices.

**MAJOR-2 (I9's licensing clause has no extraction basis).** Anchor: :246,
"a change to S_i is licensed only by a later append that grounds it and is
referenced by it, so the status history is itself carried in the appended
sequence". E10-C05 states the fixed four-field form, the single serialising
writer, and corrections-as-entries; it states no status-mutation semantics.
The handoff note (:28-:29) and trace map (:409) assert the invariants
formalise the prose specification and nothing beyond it; I9's second half
exceeds it (GR-9). *What satisfies:* weaken I9 to what E10-C05 carries
(STATUS as the designated mutable field of the fixed form, corrections filed
as entries per I8), or obtain an A1 widening of E10-C05 from a governing
line that states the licensing rule, then keep I9 unchanged.

**MAJOR-3 (the transfer hypothesis is falsifiable in name, escapable in
practice).** Anchor: :356. Three defects. (a) "first sustained operating
period" is unoperationalised, so any null re-attributes to insufficient
operation. (b) "operates the components as specified" has no adoption
criterion, the second escape hatch. (c) Criterion (i) contains an
unmeasurable counterfactual ("it would otherwise have recorded as green")
and conflates the occurrence of vacuous defects with the capacity to catch
them: a competent adopting team that never writes a vacuous checker
falsifies the hypothesis without touching the method. *What satisfies:*
operationalise the period in method-native units (a fixed number of
artifacts driven through the full ladder, or gate transits); define "as
specified" as satisfaction of a named subset of I1-I12, which the paper
already possesses and which is exactly what design invariants are for;
restate (i) as an observable (a vacuity finding filed against a checker, or
a checker failing a deliberately corrupted input at adoption test) with the
counterfactual clause deleted; state what a null on (i)-(iii) severally
versus jointly would mean.

**MAJOR-4 (the record is the evidence and its availability is never
stated).** Anchor: §6 at :338, which concedes "no external audit of the
record has been performed" but never says whether the register, ledger, and

referee question after the evidence-class concession: self-report is
tolerable exactly insofar as the record is inspectable. The paper knows how
to state withholding (the circuit-workshop fence, :290, :348); the primary
instance receives no availability statement at all. *What satisfies:* one
§6 paragraph stating the availability status of each record instrument
(public, available on request, or withheld with reason), consistent with
the one-URL claim discipline (:379); the decision on what may be promised
is first-person/A0, not the drafting seats'.
