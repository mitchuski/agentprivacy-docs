**Summary (three sentences).** The paper proves a correct and well-motivated conditional linear cap (Theorem 5.1) on transcript leakage for sequentially composed budget-constrained agents, with genuine necessity constructions for its two central hypotheses and an unusually honest decomposition discipline separating what the architecture licenses from what must be declared and expires. Its headline framing also needs repair: the "exponential-to-linear separation" of the title compares an upper bound with an upper bound, with no attainability result for the geometric regime and with budget parameters of different operational strength on the two sides.

### Findings

**BLOCKING-1. Proposition 4.2 is false as stated; the proof assumes an unstated hypothesis.**
Passage: section 4 preamble, "Throughout this section the composition is Definition 3.5 in full generality (carried state permitted, contexts possibly assembled from upstream outputs)"; Proposition 4.2, "In the model of Definition 3.5 with O_i = Z_i (full passthrough) and per-agent constraint I(O_i; S_i) <= eps_i, suppose additionally bounded contamination"; proof, "Given S_i, the emission O_i is a measurable function of (O_{i-1}, N_i) with N_i independent of (S_{1:i}, O_{i-1})".
Objection: under Definition 3.5, O_i = f_i(Z_{i-1}, rho_{i-1}, S_i, N_i); with carried state permitted, O_i is not a function of (O_{i-1}, N_i) given S_i, and the conditional-Markov step fails. Counterexample inside the stated model: N = 2, S_1 and S_2 independent uniform bits. Invocation 1 draws fresh uniform junk U (a component of N_1), emits O_1 = Z_1 = U, and retains rho_1 = S_1. Invocation 2 emits O_2 = Z_2 = rho_1 = S_1. Every stated hypothesis holds with eps_1 = eps_2 = 0: I(O_1; S_1) = 0, I(O_2; S_2) = I(S_1; S_2) = 0, and bounded contamination holds since I(O_1; S_2 | S_1) = 0 <= I(O_1; S_1) = 0. Yet L_2 = I(O_2; S_1, S_2) = H(S_1) = 1 > 0 = Sigma_i 2^{N-i} eps_i. The proposition's conclusion fails. Separately, the proof's "N_i independent of (S_{1:i}, O_{i-1})" is nowhere among the section's stated hypotheses (section 4 does not invoke ER-3).
The same gap infects the following paragraph: "the same computation yields the additive recursion L_i <= L_{i-1} + eps_i even without erasure, for the final-output adversary". The identical construction (exogenous independent contexts, O_1 junk, rho_1 = S_1, O_2 = rho_1) satisfies exogeneity and zero marginal budgets and gives I(O_2; S_{1:2}) = H(S_1) > 0, so the additive recursion also fails "without erasure". The phrase "even without erasure" is exactly wrong.
What satisfies: add to Proposition 4.2's hypotheses (i) rho_i = ⊥ for all i, equivalently O_i = f_i(O_{i-1}, S_i, N_i), and (ii) N_1, ..., N_N mutually independent and jointly independent of (S_{1:N}); replace "even without erasure" with "under the same no-carried-state hypothesis". With these the proof is correct as written (I re-derived it: the identity expansion and the final drop of I(O_{i-1}; S_i) are exact). Note the narrative improves rather than weakens: the fixed proposition says marginal budgets amplify exponentially even WITH erasure, and section 5.5 already shows that without erasure nothing bounds anything, which is a cleaner two-regime story. Loops to A3.

**MAJOR-1. The headline "separation" compares two upper bounds, and its two eps parameters are not the same object.**
Passage: title, "an Exponential-to-Linear Separation under Structural Context Erasure"; abstract, "the budget discipline the empirical literature measures (marginal, output-only constraints) admits exponential amplification"; sections 1 and 7, "bounded only by the geometric sum"; Remark 5.7, "At N = 2 the uniform comparison is 3eps against 2eps; at N = 5, 31eps against 5eps; the ratio (2^N - 1)/N grows without bound".
Objection, prong (a): Theorem 4.1 is an upper bound. No construction in the paper, and none cited, attains leakage Omega(2^N eps) under marginal budgets; the source's own empirics (0.49 to 1.05 over depths two to five) are nowhere near geometric. A separation in the standard sense needs a lower bound on the bad regime; as written, the paper separates best-available guarantees, not achievable leakages, and "admits exponential amplification" asserts attainability that is not established. Prong (b): the eps on the two sides have different operational semantics. A conditional interface-inclusive budget at level eps is a strictly stronger constraint on the implementation than a marginal output-only budget at the same numeral: Proposition 4.3's own system has every marginal budget equal to zero while its conditional budget is H(S_2). Comparing 31eps with 5eps as if eps were a common unit is not like-with-like, and Remark 5.7's two fences (discipline-versus-mechanism; empirical conformance) do not include this one.
What satisfies: either exhibit or cite an attainability construction for the geometric regime, or reframe: the true unconditional separation the paper already owns is Proposition 4.3 (zero budget versus total transcript recovery, an infinite separation), and the exponential-versus-linear comparison should be stated as a comparison of guarantees under stated budget semantics, with one added sentence in Remark 5.7 conceding that the conditional budget at level eps is the more demanding constraint. Title survives if the abstract and Remark 5.7 carry the fence. Loops to A3 (statement) and A2 (abstract, sections 1/7 wording).

**MAJOR-2. ER-6's time-indexed budgets are ill-typed against Shannon mutual information, and Corollary 5.4's expiry narrative inherits the seam.**
Passage: ER-6, "All budgets and capacities are evaluated against a stated decoder class at a stated time t; every eps_i is eps_i(t), non-decreasing in t under growth of the class"; Corollary 5.4, "With budgets eps_i(t) non-decreasing in t, the composed shelf life is t*_lin = sup{t : Sigma_i eps_i(t) < H(X)}".
Objection: ER-5's budget I(O_i, Z_i; X | Z_{i-1}) is a functional of the joint law alone. It does not depend on any decoder class, and no growth of adversary capability changes it. Read literally, if the declared eps_i genuinely bound the Shannon quantities then Theorem 5.1's bound and Corollary 5.4's floor hold against all decoders forever, and nothing expires; conversely, if eps_i(t) are class-relative effective-information quantities, then the chain rule and data-processing steps of Theorem 5.1's proof are not available for them (class-relative information measures do not satisfy the chain rule in general), and the theorem as proved does not apply. There is a coherent third reading, which I believe is the intended one: the eps_i(t) are declared, certified upper bounds on fixed but unknown Shannon quantities, whose certification (audit) is only as strong as the decoder class used to test it at time t, so what grows with t is the certified value, not the information. But the paper never says which reading holds, and a PoPETs reader at this seat will ask precisely this.
What satisfies: one paragraph, at ER-6 or in Remark 3.4, fixing the semantics of eps_i(t) as certified-bound values (or otherwise), and one clause in Corollary 5.4 making the expiry claim about the certification, not the mutual information. The deferral to companion work covers the formalisation of the class family, not the type of the symbol the corollary quantifies over. Loops to A3.

**MAJOR-3. ER-1's literal wording already excludes the carried-state channel, contradicting Definition 3.5 and making Proposition 5.5 self-contradictory on a literal reading.**
Passage: ER-1, "All inter-invocation information flow passes through the declared interfaces {Z_i}; no undeclared channel connects invocations"; Proposition 5.5, "satisfying ER-1, ER-3, ER-4 and ER-5 with eps_1 = eps_2 = 0", where the construction's pad rho crosses the invocation boundary with Z_1 = ⊥.
Objection: the persistent pad is inter-invocation information flow that does not pass through {Z_i}. Under ER-1 as literally worded, Proposition 5.5's composition violates ER-1, so the proposition's hypothesis set is unsatisfiable as stated and the necessity claim for ER-2 collapses into ER-1. The neighbouring sentence "ER-1 is what the functional form of Definition 3.5 encodes" points at the intended reading (ER-1 excludes channels outside the model's declared components, of which rho is one, separately killed by ER-2), but the requirement's first sentence says otherwise, and ER-1 is load-bearing for every result in section 5.
What satisfies: reword ER-1 to quantify over channels outside Definition 3.5's declared components ({Z_i} and the carried state {rho_i}), for example "no information flows between invocations except through the declared interfaces {Z_i} and the modelled carried state {rho_i}; no undeclared side channel exists", leaving the destruction of rho to ER-2 alone. One sentence; restores Proposition 5.5's coherence. Loops to A3.

**MAJOR-4. "Auditable" overclaims for ER-5; the abstract's promise is not carried by Remark 5.7's audit list.**
Passage: abstract, "under five auditable engineering requirements"; Remark 5.7 and section 6, "leakage measurement against the declared class (ER-5, ER-6)"; ER-5, "It is a per-invocation auditable quantity".
Objection: ER-1 to ER-4 are structural and plausibly auditable (channel inventory, runtime state audit, provenance analysis). ER-5 requires certifying a conditional mutual information between an LLM invocation's full emission and the protected source given the received interface. Estimating mutual information for high-dimensional text channels is a notoriously hard statistical problem; the paper cites no estimation method, states no estimator, and section 6's threats-to-validity does not name estimation error as a threat, so the word "auditable" is doing promissory work the paper does not back. ER-5's own gloss ("depends only on the joint law of one invocation's input interface, emission, and the source") establishes locality, which helps, but locality is not auditability.
What satisfies: either soften "auditable" to "declared" or "locally testable in principle" at the abstract and ER-5, or add three sentences to section 6 owning the gap: ER-5 conformance requires MI estimation, estimation is feasible only against restricted decoder classes, and this is one reason ER-6 indexes budgets by class and time. The second option is stronger and consistent with the paper's own honesty discipline. Loops to A2 (with the section 6 sentence checked by A3 for type-consistency with MAJOR-2's resolution).

**MINOR-1. One-bit threshold slippage between ER-7, Corollary 5.4's non-vacuity claim, and t*_lin.**
Passage: Corollary 5.4, "Per ER-7, this floor is non-vacuous exactly when the declared deficit Sigma_i eps_i < H(X) - 1 holds" versus ER-7's condition "Sigma_i eps_i(t) < H(X)" and, two lines later, "t*_lin = sup{t : Sigma_i eps_i(t) < H(X)}".
Objection: the corollary labels H(X) - 1 as "the declared deficit per ER-7" while ER-7 and t*_lin use H(X); the displayed floor is already vacuous strictly before t*_lin. In a paper that announces "We add only precision" (section 2), this will be noticed. What satisfies: note that exact Fano (with the binary-entropy term) gives P_e > 0 whenever Sigma_i eps_i < H(X), so the ER-7 deficit does license strict incompleteness, while the displayed linear floor is positive only when Sigma_i eps_i < H(X) - 1; two clauses reconcile all three thresholds. Loops to A3.

**MINOR-2. Fano estimator range unstated.**
Passage: Theorem 3.2, "For any estimator X̂(Y)". The log(|X| - 1) form of Fano requires X̂ to take values in the source alphabet; for estimators ranging over a superset the denominator is log|X|. Add "taking values in the alphabet of X". Loops to A3.

**MINOR-3. Definition 3.7's "Equivalently (interface sufficiency)" is an implication, not an equivalence.**
Passage: Definition 3.7. rho_i = ⊥ implies the stated conditional independence, but the converse fails (rho_i may be retained yet causally inert, or deterministic). Either write "Consequently" or define erasure by the conditional-independence property and note rho_i = ⊥ realises it. Loops to A3.

**MINOR-4. Section 1 overstates the necessity coverage.**
Passage: section 1, "The hypotheses are individually load-bearing." Necessity is demonstrated for exactly two of the five (ER-2, and ER-5's conditional form); the abstract and section 5.2 phrase this precisely, section 1 does not. Either weaken to "the two central hypotheses are individually load-bearing" or add the cheap third construction for ER-4, which exists: two invocations, O_1 = Z_1-bypassing junk N_1 emitted as O_1 with Z_1 = ⊥, downstream context assembled around the interface as S_2 = X xor N_1, O_2 = S_2; every conditional interface-inclusive budget is zero (I(N_1; X) = 0, I(X xor N_1; X) = 0) yet the transcript recovers X exactly. Loops to A2 (or A3 if the construction is adopted).

**MINOR-5. The DP contrast is fair but loosely worded (L075 surface, priced).**
Passage: section 2, "sequential composition of mechanisms degrades additively by construction, a property of the definition itself". Basic composition is a theorem, not a definitional clause; advanced composition degrades sublinearly, so "additively" is the crude bound; and the deeper reason DP composes without architectural assumptions is that its guarantee is worst-case over neighbouring inputs and adaptively chosen mechanisms, which is the honest analogue of ER-5's conditioning. One sharpened sentence both fixes the imprecision and strengthens the paper's own point. Loops to A2.

**MINOR-6. Citation-role stretch on csiszar1978broadcast.**
Passage: section 2, "that line records the classical failure mode of additive accounting for colluding observers"; ER-1, "The colluding-observer literature shows the additive structure fails without it [csiszar1978broadcast]". Csiszar-Korner 1978 treats broadcast channels with confidential messages and a single eavesdropper; it is the right lineage citation but not a colluding-observer result. The superadditivity of joint information over marginals is elementary and needs no citation; either state it as such or cite a source that actually treats colluding wiretappers. Loops to A2.

**MINOR-7. Conjecture 5.8 is existentially quantified over formalisations and is therefore a research programme, not a falsifiable conjecture.**
Passage: Conjecture 5.8, "There exists a formalisation of the N-invocation system in which ..."; Falsifier paragraph. A failed candidate site does not refute the existential, so the stated falsifier falsifies a candidate criterion, not the conjecture. To this seat the fencing is honest (proof obligations enumerated, the unstarted step named, nothing depends on it), and I would not reject over it; but the label invites the objection. Restyle as "Open Problem 5.8", or fix a candidate site so the statement becomes falsifiable. Loops to A3.

**MINOR-8. Definition 3.8's "closure of the system's permitted operations" is undefined.**
Passage: Definition 3.8. Acceptable only because no theorem depends on the grade criterion, which section 5.3 says; say it at the definition too ("the closure is left informal here; see section 5.3"). Loops to A3.

**Desk-reject verdict:** no grounds. The paper is in scope for PoPETs, the model is stated, the proofs are present and (with BLOCKING-1 repaired) correct, and the limits sections exceed venue norms.

**The single change that most improves acceptance odds:** repair Proposition 4.2's hypotheses (BLOCKING-1). It is the only false statement in the paper, it sits in the section a referee reads to calibrate trust in the harder section 5, and the fix simultaneously cleans the "even without erasure" sentence and sharpens the two-regime narrative.

**Overall recommendation, PoPETs terms: MAJOR REVISION.** With BLOCKING-1 and MAJOR-1 to MAJOR-4 resolved, this reviewer's expectation at second round is accept with minors; the mathematical core (Theorem 5.1, Propositions 4.3/5.5/5.6, Corollaries 5.2/5.4) survived a full re-derivation at this seat.
