### 1 · Pre-P1 obligation L065(b): the bounded-contamination correspondence

**VERDICT: ORTHOGONAL.** The draft's Proposition 4.2 condition and the actual hypotheses of Asif-Amiri Theorem 4.1 are formally incomparable: neither implies the other. "Matches", "stronger" and "weaker" are all ruled out.

Their hypothesis statement, quoted verbatim from arXiv:2603.05520v1 (Theorem 4.1, Cumulative Leakage Bound):

> "Consider a sequential agent pipeline satisfying the Markov structure as given in Eq. (2) and assume that the sensitive variables {S_i}_{i=1}^{N} are mutually independent. If each agent satisfies the local leakage constraint I(O_i; S_i) ≤ ε_i, then the global compositional leakage satisfies I(O_N; S_1, …, S_N) ≤ Σ_{i=1}^{N} 2^{N−i} ε_i."

with Eq. (2) being "S_{i−1} → (O_{i−1}, D_i, S_i) → O_i → O_{i+1} → ⋯ → O_N, for each i ∈ {1, …, N}", and D_i defined as "public or task-relevant inputs to agent a_i". Confirmed by inspection of their Appendix A.1: the proof runs a recursion over ℒ_i := I(O_i; S_i | S_{<i}) (their Eq. 18), reaching ℒ_i ≤ ε_i + Σ_{j<i} ℒ_j (their Eq. 25) and the closed form (their Eq. 27); mutual independence of the S_i and the conditional Markov structure are the only substantive inputs. The paper nowhere states any condition of the bounded-contamination form, and nowhere constrains the coupling between D_i and the S_j.

Why neither direction holds:

- **Their hypotheses do not imply bounded contamination.** Nothing in their model forbids a public input correlating with a later agent's secret. Take S_1, S_2 independent uniform bits and D_1 = S_2 with O_1 = D_1: mutual independence and Eq. (2) hold, yet I(O_1; S_2 | S_1) = H(S_2) > 0 = I(O_1; S_1), violating the draft's condition.
- **Bounded contamination does not imply their hypotheses.** It permits arbitrarily dependent secrets (it constrains the secret-transcript coupling given earlier secrets, not the secrets' joint law); S_2 = S_1 satisfies it in suitable constructions while violating mutual independence outright.

The two conditions close the same 2^{N−i} recursion by different routes: theirs via independent secrets and the conditional Markov step, the draft's via pricing the contamination channel directly. The draft's scope note ("neither claims the other's hypotheses") is accurate as written and needs no edit. Supporting observation, not overreach: the source's stated hypotheses leave the D_i-to-S_j coupling unconstrained, which is exactly the channel Proposition 4.2's condition prices; the draft's decision to carry its own named condition rather than claim theirs is the right one.

**Import-fidelity flag for A3 (not patched; A3 owns the mathematics).** The draft's restatement of Theorem 4.1 ("Under sequential composition of N agents with per-agent constraint I(O_i; S_i) ≤ ε_i, the final output satisfies ...") omits both source hypotheses: the Eq. (2) Markov structure and, more materially, the mutual independence of the sensitive variables, which Definition 3.5 in "full generality" does not supply. As restated, the imported theorem is stronger than the source's actual theorem. Proposed ledger entry below; one-line fix is to carry the source's two hypotheses inside the imported statement.
