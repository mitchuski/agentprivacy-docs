### Findings

**MAJOR-1 (the GR-7 passage is formally complete but illegible to the members who must vote on it).** Anchor: :231. Y_S and Y_M appear only inside "formally I(Y_S; Y_M | X) = 0" and are never defined anywhere in the paper; H(X), C_S and C_M are defined but no sentence tells a non-information-theorist what R_max or the error floor means for a deployment; the whole guarantee is one ~250-word paragraph carrying five symbols and both preconditions. The L115 P0 finding of faithfulness stands; faithfulness is not legibility, and at this venue a passage only a specialist can parse is a finding even when formally correct (the card's rubric, and the venue's reality: a member who cannot paraphrase the passage abstains, and abstention kills volunteer work items more reliably than opposition). The defect is sharpened by the paper's own thesis that separation claims should be reviewable by non-specialists. *What satisfies:* define Y_S and Y_M at first use (the two agents' respective observations); add one plain-language reading sentence inside the same passage, for example that the two channels can jointly convey at most an R_max fraction of the private state and an adversary of the stated class reconstructs it with probability at most R_max; no condition weakened, no splitting of the passage, GR-7 completeness surviving verbatim in content. A0 spot-trace of the edited passage required (it is the load-bearing passage).

**MAJOR-2 (the implementation record is unattributed and uncheckable).** Anchors: :245-:249, with :293. The three designs are described in passive, anonymous voice ("A dual-runtime browser design instantiates...", "Deployed as described at its specification date" — whose specification, dated when, available where?) with no provenance, no authorship, and no availability statement. At a standards venue, evidence no member can locate reads as "trust us", and the passive constructions can additionally be read as if these were the field's implementations rather than the proposer's own designs, which is a neutrality defect on this card's own rubric. The zero-canon-citation rule forbids citing the design documents; it does not forbid attributing them. *What satisfies:* one clause attributing the record to the proposer's own design documents (joined at release by the author block and statement of interests already on the release checklist) and one sentence stating the design documents will be made available to the working group on request; the three implementation statuses stay verbatim.

**MAJOR-3 (the declaration profile is silent on the denominator).** Anchors: :260 with :231 and :266.
