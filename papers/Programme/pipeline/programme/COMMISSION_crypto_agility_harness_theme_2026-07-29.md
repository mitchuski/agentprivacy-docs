## 1 · Scope, in one sentence

Point the adversarial agent-harness runtimes (the shor_mage / tigzkp lineage, and the programme's own dual-agent pipeline) at cryptographic agility, framed by **Mosca's inequality X + Y > Z**, so that one machine bears on both governed terms: it **measures Z** (time-to-capability) by being a moving reader against live cryptography, and it **shrinks Y** (migration time) by auto-auditing and optimising trustworthy replacement primitives under a discipline strict enough to adopt on machine evidence without a human re-deriving every line.

## 2 · The Mosca spine (the frame everything hangs on)

Canon statement (durability note §2): **X + Y > Z ⟹ already lost** — secret shelf-life (X) + migration time (Y) vs time-to-capability (Z). The harness attacks both defender-controlled levers:

- **Term Z — measure it by being it.** A sharper resource estimate / a found weakness is a *durability signal, not an attack* (**C68**, e^(−λt) reading). The harness that finds real structure in live cryptography measures Z directly instead of guessing it; the existence-leak (**C81** ~70%, and its planning corollary **C84** Z′ = Z − D(a)) is the same term sharpening on every public attestation.
- **Term Y — shrink it safely.** Crypto-agility as migration readiness is already **C70**; the held-out gate that lets a re-keying decision be *trusted* is **C69** (C13 at the validation layer); the post-quantum hedge substrate is E9-C14 (LatticeFold, "attestations age gracefully under the horizon C67/C49 plan against").

Attach band: **C67–C71 (the Horizon District / Cryptographic Mosca)**, extending the Behavioural Mosca **C49/C61**. Natural extraction owner: **E9 (proving-substrate)** for the instrument, citing **E2**'s laws (which E9 must not restate) and **E10/WP-27** for the discipline.

## 3 · Coherence guard — why this does NOT undercut "Forget beats Rotate" (CRITICAL)

Two lanes, quoted from the Moving Ceiling's "three answers":

- **Lane 1 — custody & maintainers (keys / circuits / protocols).** "**Rotate.** Crypto-agility... protects what has custody and maintainers: keys, circuits, protocols. It does nothing for data already emitted about a person. **Necessary, not sufficient.**" The theme lives *entirely* inside this sentence.
- **Lane 2 — behavioural data stays Forget.** "You cannot rotate your past." "Amnesia is the only term in the equation whose security is independent of time." Register anchors **C86** (~30%), **C17** (60%) — untouched by this theme.

The resolution to carry in every artifact: crypto-agility and Forget answer *different questions about different objects*. The harness improves Rotate **only within the custody lane**; it has nothing to offer the no-custody lane, so improving it cannot undercut the headline.

**Three contradiction risks and their phrase-arounds** (from the dream):
1. *"We improve durability" overclaim* → always tag C70 "migration readiness" + "necessary, not sufficient"; never say the harness improves *behavioural* durability.
2. *Custody laundering via the existence-leak* → keep C84 scoped: the harness's Z-measurement is a *planning service* for maintained protocols and only a *pricing signal* (not a fix) for behavioural archives.
3. *GR-1 / GR-7* → no prose states or moves a confidence; any R<1 mention is time-indexed R(t) with preconditions. Authorship stays first-person (never cede to the harness).
