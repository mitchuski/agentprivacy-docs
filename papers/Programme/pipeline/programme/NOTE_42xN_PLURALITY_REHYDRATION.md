# 42 × n · The Plurality Rehydration Method

**Verdict first: this note records the second outcome of the programme so it is not lost, and parks it. The core serious deliverable remains the paper set (Parts C, E, H of the programme). Nothing in this note enters the calendar, the manifest, or any task card until the activation conditions at the end are met and the First Person says go.**

## The two outcomes of the current direction

1. **Specific papers out of the living docs.** The pipeline as it stands: eleven extractions, twenty-seven work packages, the standards matrix, the thesis skeleton. This is the funded lane and the only one running.
2. **A plurality of complete rehydrations.** The same model reconstructed end to end by many distinct research-agent types, each type a Game-of-42 combination: one persona (from the 6-axis by 7-station board, and behind it the 42 primary personas of the skills corpus) crossed with a skill loadout (from the 166-skill operational corpus) and a register assignment (myth, mathematics, research note, blog, standards prose, and everything between). 42 combinations per configuration, times n configurations. The output surface is a wiki: one forkable page-space per combination, the same model told 42 × n ways, on the existing federation infrastructure.

## Why the second outcome is research and not content generation

The programme already treats independent convergence as evidence and divergence as a finding (Part A.3: the two-register discipline is the method, not the embarrassment). The plurality method generalises two registers to n registers and makes the discipline measurable:

- **Each rehydration is a measurement of invariance under re-expression.** A claim that survives every lens unchanged belongs to the robust core. A claim that fractures, meaning it holds in some registers and fails or mutates in others, is register-dependent, and the fracture pattern is a map of the model's honest limits. That map does not exist today and cannot be produced any other way.
- **The round-trip is the gate.** Every rehydration must compress back to the same invariant core (the understanding-compression discipline already exists as a working skill with a coherence gate). A telling that does not round-trip is not published as a variant; it is filed to the ledger as a finding against either the telling or the model. Contraction is where the model improves.
- **Fracture findings feed the register.** Divergences discovered under expansion become conjecture candidates or corrections through the normal register process, with the same prominence as confirmations (GR-8). The plurality engine is therefore an instrument pointed back at the model, and the improvement loop is: expand into combinations, contract through the gate, file the residue.
- **The method is itself a contribution.** WP-27 (conjecture governance as method) and thesis Ch.7 gain their strongest evidence from a corpus of n-register convergence data. Proof-of-understanding (the RPP primitive) gets an unusual empirical companion: each agent type demonstrably comprehends the model well enough to reconstruct it in its own register, which is the protocol's own claim exercised at scale.

## Expansion and contraction, operationally

- **Expand:** choose a combination set (a full 42-board sweep, or a targeted heptad along one axis); each agent boots with its persona card, skill loadout, and register; each produces a complete telling of the model, tracing to the same extraction layer (E1 to E11) every paper traces to. The corpus registry and ground rules bind these agents exactly as they bind the paper fleet; canon stays read-only; the register stays the sole authority.
- **Contract:** run each telling through the round-trip gate; log convergence, divergence, and silence (a claim a register cannot express at all is a datum, not a failure); merge the residue into ledger findings.
- **Surface:** the wiki federation, one subdomain or page-space per combination, forkable, with the invariant core and fracture map as the hub pages. The pipeline's chronicles record every cycle of this the way they record everything else.
- **Contain:** plurality runs are bounded batches with a fixed combination list declared before the run (the pre-registration habit applied to the method itself). No batch runs while it would compete with a work-package deadline for attention or review capacity.

## What this must never do

Dilute the flagship. The papers earn the credibility that makes a 42 × n wiki read as a research instrument rather than generated sprawl. The order is papers first, plurality as the demonstration that the method scales. The same rule the programme applies to standards (two in flight, never more) applies here as a posture: the plurality engine idles until the core lane is safely ahead of its dates.
