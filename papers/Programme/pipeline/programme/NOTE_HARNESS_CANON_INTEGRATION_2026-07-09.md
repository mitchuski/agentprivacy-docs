## 1. cityofmages (narrative canon)

The city already owns the vocabulary the harness needs — this is completion,
not invention:

- **Swordsman ⊥ Mage is the dual pair.** The skeleton's soulbis ⊥ soulbae is
  the class pairing in work clothes; the circuit workshop already ran on the
  Game-of-42 compute-axis heptad; the pipeline's builder/attacker split
  (A3 ⊥ A5) is the same ⊥ at the seat level.
- **Proposed act, Tome IX (the Horizon):** one new act — working title
  *"The Fleet That Reads Its Own Scrolls"* — recording, in city register,
  the runtime carrying the moving-ceiling work up the ladder: seats that
  draft, attack, repair, audit; the reviewer who forged a counterexample and
  had it taken into the work as a proposition; the audit that caught the
  fleet's own vacuous instruments; and the rung no construct may climb — the
  First Person's read. Tome IX is the moving-ceiling tome (C90-C93, the
  limitative reading), so the runtime that elevated that exact material
  belongs there. GATE: first-person act-binding, as with every act.
- **Proposed guild placement, not a new shop.** The harness is a *rite held
  in common*, not a twentieth workshop — adding a shop forces a grimoire
  re-pin and district geometry for what is really a discipline every shop
  can run. Natural custodians: the Archivist's Tower (Tome VIII — the
  chronicle/ledger duty is literally the Archivist's) with the Vaulting
  Guild candidate lore (wall/seal/vault triad) as the review-gate metaphor
  if the three-guilds draft binds. GATE: guild lore is draft-candidate and
  UNBOUND; binding is first-person.
- **What does NOT go in:** theorem content (fence), constraint counts or
  levers from the circuit workshop (its own fence), and any suggestion the
  fleet marks P4 — the city's version of the hard stop should be stated in
  city terms (the signature that does not multiply).
