## Summary (3 sentences)

Release-draft v5 genuinely resolves review 1's four MAJOR weaknesses: the legal bridge now runs through Articles 25(1) and 32(1)-(2) GDPR with Article 22 correctly confined to the rights outcome, every recommendation names its addressee and instrument in accordance with the AI Act's actual conformity architecture, subsection 2.1 states honestly what an assessor can and cannot check, and the three-layer reframe removes the disparagement of supervisory enforcement without weakening the structural argument. But the brief now says of its capacity-deficit condition both that "it is the quantity a deployer measures and declares" (section 2) and that the capacities are, "today, declarations rather than measurements" for which "no standardised measurement methodology ... exists" (section 2.1); a vendor's counsel will quote the first sentence against the second, and the equivocation sits exactly on the condition recommendation 2 asks assessors to demand. One MAJOR wording repair and four MINOR items; no BLOCKING; the artifact is one narrow revision from release-strip.

## Strengths (exactly 3)

1. **The brief now passes its own test end to end.** The three-condition guarantee block of section 2 (two preconditions plus the declared capacity deficit, typographically inseparable from the claim), section 3's "What expires at t* is the deficit condition, not the architecture", and recommendation 2's demand for exactly those three conditions form a coherent chain a hostile technical reader cannot pull apart on substance. The L042 decomposition survived the v5 revision intact.

2. **The legal rerouting is done properly, not cosmetically.** The Article 25(1)/32(1)-(2) classification is argued from the elements of the provisions (measures at the determination of the means; ongoing confidentiality; unauthorised disclosure or access), Article 22 is explicitly denied as decided ("This brief does not claim that the guarantee of section 2 decides whether a given decision is based solely on automated processing, or whether its effects are legal or similarly significant"), and the section 6 routing paragraph matches the AI Act as enacted: Article 43(2)/Annex VI internal control, Article 74 market surveillance with the 74(8) DPA designation, the Article 40 standardisation request, and MCC-AI correctly flagged non-binding.

3. **Subsection 2.1's explicit negative is the most useful sentence in the brief for its stated audience.** "No standardised measurement methodology for observation-channel capacities against machine-learning adversary classes exists, and this brief cites none because none is established" converts review 1's MAJOR 4 from a concealed weakness into an actionable standardisation ask (recommendation 2), and the new section 7 limit ("documentary, not metrological") carries the same honesty into the limits section.

## Weaknesses (numbered; severity BLOCKING | MAJOR | MINOR)

1. **MAJOR · The brief contradicts itself on whether the capacity-deficit condition is measured or declared, and the contradiction spans its load-bearing condition.** Three retained sentences carry measurement vocabulary: the executive summary's "hold by construction, under stated and auditable preconditions together with one measurable capacity condition"; section 2's "a capacity-deficit condition that is measurable and declarable for a given system and adversary class"; and the conditions block's heading "**Capacity-deficit condition (measurable and declarable).**" with its closing clause "it is the quantity a deployer measures and declares". Against them, subsection 2.1 states: "The quantities C_S, C_M and H(X) are, today, declarations rather than measurements" and "no standardised measurement methodology for observation-channel capacities against machine-learning adversary classes exists". The two positions are reconcilable in principle (measurable in principle; declared in current practice) but the brief never performs the reconciliation, and as drafted the vendor's counsel that review 1 invoked can quote section 2 against section 2.1 to argue that the brief equivocates on the checkability of the very condition recommendation 2 would have treated as mandatory. Trace: the measurement vocabulary is v4's, prescribed by review 1 itself (weakness 1's corrective wording, executed at L043 under L042); the declaration frame is v5's resolution of review 1 MAJOR 4 (L053, subsection 2.1). Review 2 records this as a reversal of review 1's own prescribed adverbials: they were correct against v3's defect and are stale against v5's attestation frame. Fix, either branch staying within GR-9: (a) replace the measurement vocabulary at the three points with declaration vocabulary ("one declarable capacity condition"; "declarable for a given system and adversary class"; "(declarable)" and "it is the quantity a deployer declares; section 2.1 states how the declaration is checked"); or (b) reconcile in place ("measurable in principle, declared in practice today, per section 2.1") at the first occurrence and demote the rest. Note for A0: options touch wording inside the L042-fenced block without touching the decomposition; a fence ruling is required before A2 edits (proposed via this reviewer's chronicle, not appended to the ledger, as the item is MAJOR, not BLOCKING).

2. **MINOR · The t* glosses take the first-crossing reading the sup-definition does not supply, and the support for that reading lies outside the brief's extraction basis.** Section 3 defines "t* = sup { t : R(t) < 1 }" and immediately glosses it as "the horizon up to which the stated guarantee, with its stated adversary class, remains in force"; the same section says R(t) can pass one "once C_S(t) + C_M(t) reaches H(X)"; recommendation 3 asks for "the horizon t* within which the evaluation is claimed to hold". All three glosses read t* as a first crossing, which coincides with the supremum only under monotone effective capacities. That monotonicity is available in the extraction layer (E2-C04, ordered decoder classes, per ledger L054) but E2-C04 is not in this brief's extraction basis, so the glosses currently exceed their support (GR-9) while the definitional escalation L054 remains open at the register process. The dependence is flagged today only in the strip-at-release apparatus comment, so the released text would carry it silently. Cheap fix without awaiting L054: weaken the glosses to sup-accurate wording ("the latest time at which the stated guarantee ... is still in force"; "the horizon t* to which the evaluation is claimed to hold"), or state the monotone-adversary-availability reading as an explicit assumption in section 3.

3. **MINOR · The Article 35(3)(a) paraphrase is broader than the provision.** Section 1: "Where such processing involves a systematic and extensive evaluation based on automated processing, including profiling, a data protection impact assessment is already mandatory (Article 35(3)(a))". Article 35(3)(a) additionally requires that the evaluation be of personal aspects relating to natural persons and that decisions be based on it which produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect the person. The same paragraph has just declined to decide the "solely" and "significant effects" thresholds for Article 22; silently eliding the parallel effects element of 35(3)(a) is the exact overreach shape the paragraph forswears, and a supervisory reader will notice. One-clause fix ("and on which decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are based") or a hedge ("a data protection impact assessment is typically already mandatory").

4. **MINOR · A capability claim rides under an external-citable marker.** Section 5: "Separately, IEEE 7012-2025 standardises machine-readable personal privacy terms, the contractual layer a structurally separated agent can sign and honour. [Regulatory and standards facts, external-citable]". The standard's existence and subject matter are external facts; the appositive "a structurally separated agent can sign and honour" is this brief's own synthesis and is not carried by the marker as written. Either mark the appositive as synthesis or drop it; the section loses nothing.

5. **MINOR · Stale apparatus statement (strip-at-release surface only).** The v4 handoff note still states the L042 conflation "is carried in the extraction wording of E2-c01 itself ... so it is escalated CANON-LEVEL per GR-10"; E2-C01 was re-issued to the decomposition and the spec corrected and approved (L052, L047, L056(1)), so the statement is now false as a description of the extraction layer. Already flagged at L052 ("A2/A8 refresh at next touch"); recorded here because a revision touch is now occurring. No release impact (the comment strips), no new ledger entry required.
