**Verdict first.** The literature anticipates the *phenomenon* WP-04 systematises (privacy guarantees that erode as adversary capability grows against fixed archives) in at least four independent strands: harvest-now-decrypt-later, everlasting privacy, retroactive de-anonymisation, and migration-timeline analysis. It does not anticipate the *formalisation*: no found work derives a shelf life from a capacity-to-entropy ratio R(t), isolates a declared capacity-deficit condition as the expiring component of a conditional guarantee, or orders adversary classes as a time-indexed family. The defensible novelty is therefore in the formal apparatus and the unification, never in the observation that guarantees expire. Two E2 claims (E2-C11, E2-C15) are substantially anticipated and must be framed as synthesis; no swept work *contradicts* an E2 claim, so no CONTESTED tag is raised. Precision notes for A0 are listed before the ledger.

## Precision notes for A0 (no CONTESTED items raised)

Nothing swept contradicts an E2 claim; GR-10 is not triggered. Three precision notes, proposed as ledger entries in my chronicle:

1. **E2-C07 metric and attribution.** The primary record is Babbush et al. (arXiv:2603.28846, Google Quantum AI with Ethereum Foundation and Stanford co-authors), posted 2026-03-30/31. The "~10x" is the paper's own claim *in spacetime volume over best prior single-instance estimates*; secondary coverage variously reports "roughly 20x fewer resources", and Schrottenloher reports factor 2-3 per axis (gates, qubits) against Litinski. The SoK should state the metric explicitly when repeating the figure. Enrichment, not contradiction.
2. **E2-C11 anticipation.** The everlasting-privacy and everlasting-security lines (Moran-Naor 2006; Aumann-Ding-Rabin 2002; Unruh 2013; Haines et al. 2023) own the observation that only unconditional protection survives capability growth. E2-C11's novelty is confined to the placement of an *erasure* term inside the R(t) decomposition and the Grade-1/Grade-2 naming; the naming must be presented as terminology.
3. **E2-C15 anticipation.** Harnik-Pinkas-Shulman-Peleg (2010) anticipate the existence-confirmation mechanism, including the guessable-candidate scope limit. The unclaimed residue is the cross-system corroboration monotonicity and the subsumption under the existence-leak law. A3 must cite this work in any statement of the claim.

## Novelty ledger

### Assertions defensible as NEW (state each no wider than written here)

- **N1 (traces to E2-C02).** The definition of a reconstruction ratio R(t) = (C_S(t) + C_M(t))/H(X) with capacities evaluated against the strongest adversary class available at time t, and shelf life t* = sup{t : R(t) < 1} as a quantity *derived from* an architecture and adversary class. Prior art states lifetimes as declared requirements (Mosca's x; NIST cryptoperiods; HNDL lifetime-vs-horizon comparisons); none derives shelf life from a capacity-to-entropy ratio.
- **N2 (traces to E2-C01).** The decomposition separating what the two preconditions license (additive capacities, Fano error floor) from what the declared capacity-deficit condition licenses (the strict bound R_max < 1), with the deficit condition identified as the *expiring* component. The wiretap lineage owns the floor machinery; no found work isolates a declared, measurable deficit condition as the time-indexed part of a privacy guarantee.
- **N3 (traces to E2-C04).** The formalisation programme: adversary classes as an ordered family {D_t}, D_t ⊆ D_t' for t ≤ t', from which monotonicity of R(t) is structural and only the rate is empirical. Unclaimed in QIF (adversary models unordered), in continual-observation DP (adversary fixed, releases grow), and in the resource-estimate literature (points, not an ordered process). Contingent on A3's grounding in the gain-function framework.
- **N4 (traces to E2-C08).** The existence-leak law as a stated inequality, I(feasibility; method) > 0, scoped to capability attestations, with the Garg-Jain-Sahai impossibility as floor and the 2026 reconstruction episode as instance. The episode is public record and the qualitative point was made informally in contemporaneous commentary; the *formalised law* and its scope fence are unclaimed. Present the law as new, the phenomenon as observed.
- **N5 (traces to E2-C09).** The attestation discount on the Mosca horizon, Z_b' = Z_b − D(a): public feasibility attestations shorten the effective horizon independently of any actual attack.
