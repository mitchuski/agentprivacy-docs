1. **The one substantive (non-cosmetic) item is WP-07.** `rehydrations/academic/linear_cap_paper.md` states its R(t) time-drift on *decoder-class / channel capacity* (compute-flavoured, precedent = fading wiretap channel), and ER-6's semantics **holds the true mutual information constant** ("only the certified value grows") — a certification-epistemics clock, not an adversary-reconstruction clock. The economics now need R(t) to be **adversary-informational-capability erosion of a fixed archive**. These measure different objects. This is a THEORY reconciliation for A3 and the First Person, not a wording fix, and it **sharpens the standing M3 blocker**: WP-07 must not merely be citable, its R(t) must measure adversary reconstruction under growing linkage, or the WP-14 durability leg does not stand even with a citable companion. **Resolve this first; the rest of evolution 3 depends on what R(t) is agreed to measure.**

3. **The largest wording debt is evolution 3, and it has a single source.** The whole moving-ceiling chain inherits its compute-flavoured drift from one definition: `extractions/E2-moving-ceiling.md` E2-C02. Re-pin E2-C02 to informational capability and the chain (SoK, essay, policy, spec §5.5, Tome IX, spellweb c82) inherits the fix. And evolution 4 (the land/soil analogy itself) is a GAP: no paper carries it yet; the rentier economics is present as "market-position rent," but the soil/land figure and the "guarantee-is-the-ownability" bridge are unwritten.
