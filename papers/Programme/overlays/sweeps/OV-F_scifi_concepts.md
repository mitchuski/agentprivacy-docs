# OV-F · Sci-Fi Concepts

The speculative furniture of the V6 programme: each concept named, tied to
the formal result it dramatises, and read for the research it CASTS. The
structural finding of this sweep: almost nothing here is idle decoration.
Twenty-two of the twenty-six concepts below have a named work package,
conjecture proof obligation, or instrument queued against them; the
programme's fiction is a forward map wearing a costume. The four that
remain uncast are flagged as such in their casts-forward lines — they are
the cheapest unclaimed paper seeds in the corpus.
