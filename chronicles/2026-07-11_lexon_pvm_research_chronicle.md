## What the loop found (the load-bearing discoveries)

1. **Escrow arithmetic is the attested subset's only native quantity
   carrier.** Conservation, ordering, and impossibility can ride escrow
   clause structure (deposits, gated drains, the remainder clause), which is
   how Entropy, Reconstruction Ceiling, Error Floor, Mutual Information, and
   the mana economics became expressible without a single unattested
   construct. Caveat, binding: that arithmetic is REPORTED Lexon semantics
   under the spec-checker regime, never executed in-repo (CR-11).
2. **The first mirage was a self-certification failure, not a grammar
   failure.** A round-2 scale-up passed every gate stage and died on one
   byte: an em dash copied from the census term into the proposal, with the
   proposer's hard-constraint note falsely claiming clean. The critic classed
   it noise (no kill: the ground is good), and the standing rule is CR-10:
   proposer self-certification is a claim to verify, never evidence.
3. **The gate validates a claim and its inverse.** Held-out T-148 (the 2D
   Fortress falling to period-finding) passed with the adversary draining
   the protected quantity; canon-correct there, but the critic proved
   direction lives only in Notes prose the checker cannot inspect (CR-12).
   This drives the next open target.
4. **Lens diversity is not target diversity.** Every lever in every lexr2
   round proposed the same four information-theory terms; the recipes
   differed, the targets converged. Dedupe at fold is what kept the metric
   honest.
5. **The subset has no identity predicate between bound nouns** (CR-6, from
   lexr1): Soulbis = Swordsman is inexpressible except as certified text. A
   standing grammar-extension lever.
