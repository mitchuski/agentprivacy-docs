## What changed for the canon

The Privacy Value Model canon has, until now, lived as prose: glossaries,
papers, conjecture register, story. Readable, but nothing could mechanically
CHECK a claim in it. Over six days (2026-07-07 planning seeds to 2026-07-12)
the dual-agent harness expressed **189 of the 211 frozen canon census terms**
in a controlled legal-language subset (Lexon, Diedrich) where the load-bearing
structural claim is machine-verifiable. Coverage-debt 211 to 22. The census is
90 percent covered.

Census note: the 211-term census was frozen deterministically from
`reference/GLOSSARY_MASTER_v4_0.md` term headings plus the
`reference/promise_theory_reference_v1_4.md` concept-mapping rows. The census
is the canon's index, not its whole body (the conjecture register, the V6
model objects, the papers, and the Tomes are not censused); coverage is over
that index.

## The load-bearing result: privacy-as-architecture is now checkable

Agentprivacy's central thesis is that privacy is architectural, "cannot, by
design," not "promises not to." In the Lexon grammar this became a mechanical
property. Each term's expression carries one of four structural claim types
(a gate, an ordering, a conjunction, an absence), and every claim is verified
by a mutation probe: the tool builds the claim's minimal structural negation
as a twin that still passes the base grammar gate, and requires the claim to
fail on the twin. An absence claim is therefore a route that provably does not
exist. The separation bound (nothing routes between the two agents), the
entropy remainder (residual uncertainty survives any observation), a fortress
falling to a quantum adversary (direction claimed as an absence): all are now
claims that survive their own negation, not prose.

## What has not crossed, and why (the honest residue)

Twenty-two terms remain. Sixteen are ordinary residue one more loop sweep
should reach. Six are conjecture-register bundle terms whose canon titles
carry conjecture numbers and confidence percentages that no automated seat is
permitted to write (the register is operator-write-only); expressing them
needs an operator naming convention and is deliberately reserved. Per-term,
coverage means the verified STRUCTURE, not the substance: the entropy
expression carries the formula's shape, not its logarithms, and each entry
names what it omits. The rule the reflection settled on holds: the argument of
the model is in constrained language; the deep frontier and the myth stay
home.
