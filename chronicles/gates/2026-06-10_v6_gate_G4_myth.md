## Candidate 1 · working title: *The Orchard Wound*

**Proposed home:** a City act instancing the incident; tome assignment yours (the capstone reviews suggested it as a convergence entry; Tome IX would set it beside the Horizon material).

The shielded pool was the proudest privacy in the realm: what moved within it, no eye could follow. A craftsman found the flaw the day after the new lens was forged, and with it he minted coin that no one could ever have counted, because the pool's own virtue was that nothing in it could be counted. The wound and the gift were one property seen from two sides. The realm patched the flaw in five days at a numbered block, and the chroniclers wrote the honest sentence: there is no way, using only the pool's own magic, to know whether the wound was ever used. Privacy as value and privacy as risk are the same structural fact, and the dragon looks both ways.

The seal held perfectly: no line of the path leaked. And yet within two months a scholar walked the same path alone, because the seal itself had spoken: it said the mountain opens, it said roughly where, and every searcher in the world stopped digging anywhere else. The wizards of the Horizon District keep the lesson by the gate: a proof of possibility is a map with one landmark, and one landmark is enough.
