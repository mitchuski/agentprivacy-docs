demonstration, not rulings. The chart was syntax-checked but not viewed.

## The inversion

The plan said "signed keys". A key whose name is its content cannot carry its own signature without renaming itself;
so the signature is set down beside the key, over the name — and the signed thing is exactly what a Verifiable Trust
Agent publishes.
