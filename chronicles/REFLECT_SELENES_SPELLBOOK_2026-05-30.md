## The two reflections

## Way Two — the Ascent: the seat docs should keep

The book gives this repo a new obligation: **keep the grimoire falsifiable against the architecture.** A poem is allowed to be myth, but its underlying claim must trace to a real document — otherwise the suite's literary edition drifts from its research edition, and the two corpora silently diverge.

Concretely, the seat to keep:

1. **Index the spellbook as a first-class corpus.** Add it to `SECOND_PERSON_TOMES_INDEX_v1.md` and `GLOSSARY_MASTER` as *Selene's Spellbook — privacymage grimoire* (Mitchell Travers / @privacymage), first published on `sync.soulbis.com`. It is the published literary face of everything else indexed here.

2. **Stand up a poem ↔ concept ↔ doc ledger.** One small table, kept here, mapping each bound poem to its source concept to its source document. The Way-One table above is the seed. Its job is to catch the moment a *new* poem introduces a term with no document behind it.

3. **Hold Movement Two open.** Rows V–VII above are descent-complete but **ascent-open** — the poems are still being revised. The ledger should mark them `provisional`. Do **not** mint new glossary terms or doc anchors *from* those poems until they settle; instead, when a new Movement-Two-onward poem needs a concept, write (or point to) the **doc first**, then let the poem render it. Doc precedes verse. That ordering is the whole discipline.

---

## What not to do

Do not retro-edit existing whitepapers to "match" the poems' phrasing. The book renders the docs; the docs do not chase the book. The only edits this reflection invites are *additive*: an index entry, a ledger, and (later, per-poem) a source doc for any genuinely new concept Movement Two introduces.
